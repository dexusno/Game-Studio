## 1. Whisk Rush — hands-on drink service

**Player promise:** handle a tiny rush-hour drinks counter with your own hands. Whisk one shared batch, pour colorful layered drinks, and decide which cup deserves the remaining fresh foam before the queue gets impatient. The trend suggests the materials and recipes; the enduring loop is dexterity, preparation, triage, and recovering a messy service.

This is an active service game. Choose a limited menu and starting ingredients, then work a short real-time shift. A good whisk-and-release motion makes a small batch that serves several cups but loses quality if it waits. Pour to a visible fill line, combine one flavor, and slide the cup to the right customer. Preparing ahead makes bursts manageable but risks waste. Earnings fund one useful equipment choice between shifts, such as an extra cup slot versus a larger bowl. Planning inventory and preparation competes with actually serving; it is not a static recipe-matching puzzle.

**First ten seconds:** a whisk makes a bright green swirl, one batch is split into two clear cups, and the player saves a nearly-lost order by choosing the ready cup rather than beginning another batch. Banana or hojicha can be one recipe without carrying the entire name or product promise.

## 2. Good Dog Shift — active enrichment daycare

**Player promise:** run one small dog-play yard through a lively afternoon. Throw a toy to redirect the energetic dogs, refill an activity station to occupy another, and escort a tired or overwhelmed dog to a quiet corner before the next group arrives. Progress comes from recognizing personalities and creating a smooth play/rest rhythm, with expressive canine reactions as the reward.

The source-to-game connection is the attention now given to enrichment and pet activities. It does not require a branded feeder or claims about real training outcomes. The evergreen loop is managing several autonomous creatures with limited time, space, and supplies while directly participating in play.

**Active decisions:** the player moves an attendant and aims throws, refills stations, tops up water, and guides dogs through fixed yard zones. One exciting toy attracts several dogs and changes their energy; a calmer station buys time but has limited uses. The same action can help a boisterous dog and overwhelm a shy one. Between short shifts, choose a small equipment improvement and which mix of fictional temperaments to accept. This is continuous attention/resource management; static placement alone cannot solve a shift.

**First ten seconds:** two dogs race after a thrown ball while a third carries it the wrong way; the player opens space for a quieter dog to reach the activity station. A funny, understandable response to the player's action must be visible before adding any business progression.

## Handoff

Whisk Rush is the more dependable production envelope; Good Dog Shift adds meaningful variety and a stronger character-reaction opportunity at higher implementation risk. Both would still need to be worthwhile without their trend associations. Exact snapshot IDs: **2649080, 1847510, 3683770, 946720, 1207480**. Compare the candidate scopes and falsifiers with the other branches before selecting anything. These notes own neither a new budget nor a final genre choice.
