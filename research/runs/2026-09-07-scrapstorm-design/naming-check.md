# Provisional title check

Access date: **2026-09-07, Europe/Oslo**. Scope: naming evidence for the design revision only. Input: the coordinator's description of an active-aim, top-down arcade game in which a magnetic toy robot throws, moves around, and recalls six conserved shield/ammo pieces. Short combat runs; no crafting. No game selection, budget change, publication, or playtest occurred in this check.

**Recommendation — hypothesis:** use **Sixfold Recoil** as the working title, subject to the coordinator's title decision. The compound had no exact-title game in the results inspected, and its closest observed name neighbours were less mechanically similar than those for Snapback Salvage or Rivet Recoil. “Sixfold” connects to the six conserved pieces; “Recoil” suggests physical combat. This is a naming judgment, not measured audience response. The title still needs throw–move–recall copy: players could interpret recoil as gun kickback. Sixfold Salvage is a viable reserve, but “salvage” may imply collection, repair, or crafting that the game does not offer.

## Limits and next useful check

The initial Steam/itch search opens in the web tool returned non-retryable retrieval errors; ordinary public HTTP supplied the observations above. Search results are fuzzy and can change with indexing, region, hidden content, or unpublished listings. itch explicitly hid sensitive content; its Scrap/Rivet results extended beyond the first page inspected. Steam's excluded results were not inspected. No private catalog, trademark register, or exhaustive spelling-variant search was performed. A negative search is **not legal clearance**. Confidence is high in the verified Scrapstorm collision and moderate in the relative working-title recommendation; exclusivity remains unestablished.

At the next already planned owner review, show Sixfold Recoil beside a short throw–move–recall description or gameplay clip and ask what action the title suggests. Reconsider if it repeatedly suggests six weapons or recoil-based movement. Refresh the exact-title searches before public naming. No additional naming research is needed for this design-only handoff.
