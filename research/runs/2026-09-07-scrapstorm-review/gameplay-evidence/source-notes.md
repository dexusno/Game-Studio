## Selection and capture method

The sample deliberately combines retrieval mechanics (Titan Souls, Boomerang X), small premium action packages (Akane, Swarmlake, Devil Daggers), a weaker adjacent mastery game (CROSSBOW: Bloodnight), a cheap build-heavy competitor (SNKRX), and the brief's low-response arena references (Spellbound Survivors, Orbital Arena). This is a purposive comparison, not a representative sample of Steam releases. First-person games differ materially from Scrapstorm's top-down controls. Neither weak review response nor a low aggregate score establishes financial failure.

[steam-comparators.json](steam-comparators.json) was captured with the existing studio Steam snapshot command. Its direct Steam API URLs request **US / USD**, regular list and current advertised prices separately, and **all-language, Steam-purchase, positive plus negative review totals**. Off-topic filtering remains the Steam API default. These are reviews, not paid units, players, wishlists, realized prices or revenue. Browser store pages returned mixed currencies/language filters; their prices and headline review totals were not substituted into the standardized snapshot. One-review differences between live surfaces are preserved rather than combined.

Review sampling used the public [Steam review endpoint](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/getreviews): for each of seven games, the four newest reviews marked positive and four newest marked negative, language=english, purchase_type=steam, filter=recent, num_per_page=4. Spellbound returned only three negative reviews, producing **55 retrieved reviews**. This intentionally oversamples adverse opinions to find failure modes; it cannot estimate their frequency. Some entries were jokes, non-specific praise, or text in a language different from the author's English setting; those were not used as substantive evidence. One Crossbow positive review was marked received_for_free despite the Steam-purchase filter; its sale recommendation is not used as paid-price evidence. No review author's hardware, bugs, playtime, authenticity or full-price purchase was independently verified.

The reproducible request pattern is:

https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/APPID?json=1&language=english&purchase_type=steam&filter=recent&num_per_page=4&review_type=positive

## Developer disclosures and source limits

- **Swarmlake:** Dominique Grieshofer's [Game Optimization and Steam Publishing for Swarmlake](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/research/publications/2018/GRIESHOFER-2018-GOS/GRIESHOFER-2018-GOS-thesis.pdf), publication year 2018, PDF page 19 / section 6, reports **21,757 units sold and USD 14,192 gross revenue as of 2018-09-30**, before listed deductions. It launched at USD 0.99 with discounts and later changed price. This is a developer report, not audited current sales or a profitability calculation. It is retained for price/receipt lessons only; its development duration is excluded from AI effort estimation.
- **SNKRX:** the developer's [post-release log](https://a327ex.com/posts/snkrx_log), entry **2021-07-09**, reports **80,781 units sold**, and attributes momentum to repeated creator coverage. These are historical reported sales, not a review multiplier or a forecast for Scrapstorm. Do not adopt the writer's speculative Steam-algorithm explanations as verified platform facts. The earlier review's development-duration comparison from the author's launch post has been removed; historical human production time does not estimate this studio's AI work.
- Searches for Akane and Boomerang X paid-unit disclosures did not locate an adequately clear primary total in this bounded review. Their paid units and profitability remain **unknown**. DANG's reported 2023 closure was about missing next-project funding; it is not used to prove Boomerang X lost money.
