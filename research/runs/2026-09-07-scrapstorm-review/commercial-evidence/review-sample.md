# Targeted player-review sample

Accessed **2026-09-07 Europe/Oslo** through Steam's public `appreviews` endpoint. For each game, requested `json=1&language=english&purchase_type=steam&review_type=negative&filter=recent&num_per_page=3`, then the identical query with `review_type=positive`. No playtime minimum; sort is recent within polarity. Returned **25 reviews**: Akane 6, SNKRX 6, Boomerang Battles 3, Spellbound Survivors 6, Rocket Fist 4. All returned entries were marked Steam purchases and not received for free. Endpoint counts in the separate snapshots use **all languages** and must not be conflated with this English sample.

This is a diagnostic sample designed to expose friction, not a representative sentiment survey. Negative reviews are deliberately overrepresented. Dates and minutes refer to the review metadata at capture; edits can describe later experiences. No current bug reproduction was attempted. Descriptions below are brief paraphrases, not review quotations. Entries excluded from thematic use remain listed so exclusions are visible.
