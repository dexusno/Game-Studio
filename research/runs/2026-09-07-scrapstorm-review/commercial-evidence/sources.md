# Commercial evidence and source limits

Accessed **2026-09-07 Europe/Oslo** unless a different date is explicitly given. UTC snapshot timestamps on September 6 correspond to September 7 locally. Source types: **observed** = visible source fact; **reported** = author/developer/player assertion; **hypothesis** = this review's interpretation. No sales estimates, review multipliers, private analytics or competitor hands-on tests were used.

**Correction on September 7:** Klaus states that there is no hard development-time limit and reports the studio's Breakout game was made in a couple of hours in an evening. The prior 6–7-day/4–6-hour figures are planning hypotheses, not owner caps. Human developer durations from the sources below are excluded from the corrected AI implementation assessment. Profitability means a credible case for realized proceeds exceeding relevant actual costs, alongside enjoyable play; a small copy count is not automatically success.

## Interpretation boundaries

This set can establish advertised alternatives, actual historical paid demand in a compact game, audience expectations in a targeted anecdotal sample, and platform constraints. It cannot establish Scrapstorm's fun, current paid-unit demand, visual appeal in motion, realized price, discoverability, refunds, profitability or finishing time. An adjective in a store description is the developer's claim. A review is its author's experience at the stated time; the reviewer's hours do not establish all purchasers' retention. Numerical review rows are not normalized for years on sale or marketing effort.

The corrected production benchmark is local studio evidence: Klaus's reported Breakout evening and the coordinator's inspection of current NEON BREACH beta-4 README/validation documentation. The current docs describe twenty campaign stages, upgrades, pickups, code-authored geometry/audio, settings/records and recorded packaging/interaction checks. This supports actual AI delivery capability; it neither attributes every current feature to the first couple of hours nor means this reviewer executed that game. The [coordinator audit](../coordinator-evidence/audit.md) owns the precise local provenance.

Actual incremental AI/tool spending, asset charges, owner effort, support costs and obtainable Scrapstorm sales remain unmeasured. The commercial report's cash break-even table is explicitly hypothetical sensitivity: its cost/contribution inputs are not allocations, product forecasts or conversions from review counts. No revenue-share percentage, human salary cost, owner-hour rate or replacement time limit has been invented.
