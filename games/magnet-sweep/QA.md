## Representative play evidence to obtain

The first meaningful playable should expose ordinary sweeping and dumping, a deliberate aimed tug, the earned Breakaway behavior, a readable linked release and a complete delivery. Do not withhold the core expressive interaction behind an untested cleaning sequence.

Observe whether a player predicts context-sensitive input, enjoys a straightforward pull, intentionally improves a pull through geometry, understands the affected linked group, enjoys a large deposit and wants the next tool improvement. Record misunderstandings and unattractive choices rather than relying on positive remarks.

For replay, give the completed rig and two arrangements that change useful starting tangles and pull directions. Observe voluntary continuation before prompting another attempt. Test whether the forge goal motivates collection, whether leftover scrap is comfortably optional, and whether ordinary sweeping remains desirable without record pressure. A finite journey or generated layout count alone does not satisfy the replay requirement.

## Technical evidence to obtain

The production plan specifies input priority and fresh-input recovery; finite corridor and direct-only neighbor selection; single release/collection/banking per piece; safe retained cargo; completion after current feedback; lasting upgrades and interruption recovery; visible previews and readable large loads; resolution mapping, sound control and performance in an actual Windows package.

Every result needs build identity, observed behavior, method and remaining limits. Keep static checks, automated execution, audiovisual inspection and human fun judgments separate. No test has passed yet.
