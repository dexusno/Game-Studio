# Asset provenance

The 2026-09-12 rebuild adds 19 original stereo sound assets and a 96-second original instrumental arrangement, generated by GenerateReworkAudio.py. Exact recipes, file hashes, diagnostics and the audio listening limitation are recorded in audio/rework/PROVENANCE.md. The material generator now includes restrained procedural world-space surface variation, with no downloaded texture. WorkbenchScene.cpp uses the engine's DaylightAmbientCubemap for ambient fill/reflection in the cooked scene; its engine-content status is recorded in manifest.csv.

Seven original Blender meshes and nine original procedural sound effects are reproducible from the game scripts. Their generators, dimensional/audio checks and source declarations are recorded beside the assets and in manifest.csv. No downloaded textures, commercial music, voice or third-party game assets are used.

CreateContent.py creates the original Unreal surface material. Runtime scene assembly uses the project meshes plus Unreal Engine basic cube/cylinder geometry and engine fonts; those engine resources are cooked into the game and are not offered as standalone source assets. The installed Unreal license applies to engine redistribution. Project-specific commercial distribution decisions and storefront disclosures remain outside the concept-demo task.
