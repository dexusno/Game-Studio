# Magnet Sweep — working title — decisions

- 2026-09-11 — **Owner selection:** Klaus chooses the magnet game for the first experiment and requests three game designers and a professional production planner. Scope of this turn: elaborate the design and plan production. Core requirements: engaging, fun, rewarding and replayable, with neither excessive complexity nor a shallow loop. No implementation or spending is inferred from the planning request.
- 2026-09-11 — **Planning assumptions, root:** offline Windows, fixed-view 3D using Unreal's established studio workflow; one-day initial experiment, five-to-eight intensive production days for a first commercial candidate, roughly four-to-six owner hours. These are provisional estimates, not evidence of feasibility or owner-mandated exact counts. Polarity, exact upgrades, run structure and final title remain design proposals. Target is NOK 20,000 cash profit per game, with a catalogue only after worthwhile results.

- 2026-09-11 — **Owner process requirements:** add an independent critique agent, and have all three designers exchange pitches and challenge each other directly before reaching a shared recommendation. The planner receives the reconciled design; the critic reviews the actual design and production plan. Root integrates results and records unresolved hypotheses. This expands review, not game features.

- 2026-09-11 — **Integrated design recommendation, root after three-way designer debate:** one fixed tray; held active magnet; capped planar movement; two materials with 12-space starting capacity; generous manual furnace release; three provisional 60/90/90-second batches; two guaranteed Wide Field/Deep Basket choices; score-only reward and local replay records. Full-load bonuses, polarity, wallet/shop and permanent-power grind were challenged and removed. Proposed numbers require playtests; owner has not endorsed each detail.
- 2026-09-11 — **Scope reconciliation, root:** fixed authored arrangements replace seed generation in the initial scope. The conditional paid ceiling is three work orders with three batch arrangements each on the same board. Completed-shift saves only, with clear quit warning, supersede the replay designer's batch-snapshot suggestion. A simple fill/lighting display supersedes an assembled-machine interpretation. Revisit convenience saves only if observed friction and cost justify them.
- 2026-09-11 — **Product-risk acknowledgment:** this compact timed salvage proposal shifts the original active-incremental example toward arcade replay. Existing incremental successes do not validate demand for this format. A fun short prototype and sufficient paid value are separate gates; do not inflate duration or presume replay from the number of builds.
- 2026-09-11 — **Production estimate, professional producer:** 36-54 focused production hours plus 4-10 contingency, total 40-64 (roughly five-to-eight eight-hour days). Owner attention is additional and capped at six hours, with about four planned. External waits and later support are separate. Nine arrangements require 6-10 hours including balance comparison. Revisit at the two-hour packaging checkpoint and eight-hour representative-probe cap; do not equate elapsed agent operation with estimated labor.
- 2026-09-11 — **Independent critique and correction, root:** the critic challenged Wide/Basket asymmetry, collection frustration, score saturation, whole-load returns, missing probe assets/test allocation, unrealistic participant coverage and ambiguous labor categories. Corrected the plan with an explicit eight-hour allocation including initial assets and observation; reproducible geometry, capture cadence, populations and target; pad-arrival and perceived-control checks; separate voluntary versus prompted retries; full-content versus sampled participant timing; and separate owner/calendar/support accounting. No feature catalogue was added. Static recheck supports a bounded experiment, not claims of fun, balance, paid demand or production feasibility.
- 2026-09-11 — **Governing owner correction; supersedes development-time constraints above:** Klaus states that the critique agent is to critique gameplay, how fun the game seems and potentially bad design, not development timelines. He explicitly says to forget the development timeline: it is not a consideration. Retain a simple coherent game and the requirements for engagement, fun, reward and replay; do not discard a good mechanic or preserve a shallow one to meet earlier day/hour ceilings. The critic is redoing its review around gameplay, designers are debating the findings, and the production plan will be an ordered implementation plan without schedule estimates or time-based stop gates. Prior estimates remain historical, not governing constraints. No implementation or spending is added by this correction.
- 2026-09-11 — **Gameplay redesign in response to critique (integration in progress):** the critic identified a contradiction between satisfying scrap collection and rewarding avoidance of low-value scrap. Designers and critic now recommend untimed collecting, safe retained cargo, a deliberate ring-drag/release pull with readable cascades, retained visible magnet improvements, and fresh arrangements using the same controls. Root and team prefer a visible furnace fill goal from any scrap over extracting a special prize, so ordinary sweeping matters and final-fleck cleanup remains optional. Root proposes dropping the hard cargo cap because it interrupts the new central payoff without supporting its decisions. Exact input/cascade/replay rules are being reconciled; none has been played.
- 2026-09-11 — **Revised gameplay consensus:** safe unrestricted cargo, no load slowdown or default countdown; explicit primary-button context for sweep, ring-latch/drag/release and deliberate furnace dump; finite broad preview corridor; Breakaway affects selected tangle plus directly linked neighbors in that corridor, with no recursive propagation. Any material fills a forge goal below available salvage. Keep meaningful Breakaway/reach improvements; remixed deliveries with the full rig must create fresh useful pulls. The third replay designer returned to challenge this second-round concept directly with the interaction designer and critic.
- 2026-09-11 — **Protect collecting from score pressure, root:** show positive per-action haul feedback in the baseline. Do not make largest-yank comparisons mandatory or persistent by default; they could make prior ordinary sweeping feel wasteful. Full-rig replay relies on enjoyable action and genuinely different spatial opportunities, which must be tested. After final upgrades, the furnace promises a completed salvage delivery and visible recovered metal, not another tool improvement. These choices simplify the existing loop rather than add a second mode.
- 2026-09-11 — **Final production-plan integration:** the professional producer replaced its schedule with dependency-led work packages, owned paths, a full representative interaction, actual gameplay observations, state/persistence contracts, assets, independent technical QA and packaging. No development-time constraint remains. Root accepts preservation of the current delivery and cargo across save/resume. Root resolves surplus continuation with “Pour & next delivery,” reusing the full deposit payoff before transition; collected scrap is never silently discarded. The gameplay critic remains focused on gameplay. No build or human playtest is claimed.

- 2026-09-12 — **Owner implementation authorization:** start developing the game and stop at the playable concept demo. The owner reiterates access to all available tools and full computer control; ask only for genuinely missing essentials. Root chooses the scoped Unreal implementation to test the actual 3D interaction; no deadline, full production or publication is inferred.

- 2026-09-12 — **Owner visual direction request:** Klaus asks whether creative artists have rendered a graphics style to improve appeal. An art director creates an explicitly labeled concept image and reviews the actual package. Root applies camera, tray/material, furnace depth and HUD fixes to the playable scene. Independent QA also reviews readability. The target is a tactile salvage toy with teal enamel, distinct metals and a warm copper furnace. Concept art is not gameplay evidence or a sales prediction; no new gameplay systems are added for the image.
- 2026-09-12 — **Implementation correction from native evidence:** frame polling misclassified a fast drag from a ring as a sweep. The game now captures press/release directly from viewport events and sweeps across the traveled segment; simple press context stays fixed until release. This implements the existing contract rather than changing the design.

- 2026-09-12 — **Verified concept milestone:** independently exercised both improvements and contrasting arrangements in the Windows package, plus retained cargo, whole-haul/surplus credit, save/relaunch and full-rig retry. Repaired native gesture, tray-edge collection, reward visibility and fullscreen restoration defects. Stop at the concept demo for owner judgment of feel, sound and voluntary replay; no commercial or enjoyment claim follows from functional checks.
