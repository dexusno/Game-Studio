# Mayors — three tiers of permanent upgrades

18 September 2026. See the [complete upgrade catalogue](UPGRADE-CATALOGUE.md), [searchable catalogue](UPGRADE-CATALOGUE.html) and [upgrade system](UPGRADE-SYSTEM.md). Individual effects and numbers are proposals for beta balancing.

## Selected role

At the beginning of each City, its Mayor offers **three Rare or Legendary permanent upgrades; choose one**. The acquired item lasts for the current campaign and installs on acceptance. Mayors are civic allies, not bosses. Their gifts must be strong, with progressively stronger offerings across the three city attack waves. The owner specifically names STS2's Ancient relics as the inspiration for these gifts.

The new **102-item Mayor pool** replaces the earlier nine-item demonstration. It has **34 entries per city tier: 22 shared and 3 for each of the four mercenaries**. A particular mercenary therefore has 25 potentially eligible gifts at each city entry. Innate character abilities are separate and remain active. No automatic city-entry healing is added; an upgrade may explicitly heal or grant maximum HP under its own rule.

## Power progression

| Tier | Intended impact | Illustrative design direction | Rarity mapping |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| City 1 — establish the build | Strong opening reliability and a reason to prefer a strategy. | Prepared ammunition/protection, a resource delivery, Shield retention, better Precision, a character's opening engine. | Rare or Legendary; a significant default exception can be Legendary even in City 1. |
| City 2 — make the engine repeatable | Recurring benefits supporting a larger recipe collection and harder robot combinations. | Extra no-cooldown recipe use with full costs, additional cooling, repeated material supply, another Precision attempt, extra recipe memory. | Rare or Legendary according to impact, not an automatic rarity promotion for changing cities. |
| City 3 — change late-wave decisions | A large payoff, strong protection or a substantial rule exception useful during the remaining wave. | Late manufacturing engines, large character synergies, stronger resource conversion and survival tools with explicit boundaries. | The strongest effects belong at Legendary; compare cumulative owned gifts as well as the new choice. |

These are intended power bands, not measured equivalence or a promise that every late gift dominates every earlier one in every build. Existing gifts stay owned after travelling. Do not compensate secretly by changing rolled enemy stats after the player chooses an upgrade.

## Offer construction proposal

1. Select only this city's tier. Filter by the current mercenary or Shared, then remove owned IDs and aliases. Mayor-exclusive items cannot appear in ordinary shops, Officer loot or Mystery rewards, so earlier acquisition cannot drain a future tier.
2. Build three distinct choices: one shared infrastructure/supply option, one shared tactical option and one mercenary-specific option where all resolve legally. If a role has no eligible candidate, use another eligible shared option. Do not hide an unusual choice merely because the current build is not optimal for it.
3. Resolve acquisition requirements before offering an item. When an effect asks for recipe selections, offer only legally selectable copies and show the available count. The pool must still supply three valid choices; use eligible shared alternatives instead of an impossible or duplicate choice.
4. Seed the ordered candidates and nested offers. Reopening the screen does not reroll it. Accepting one records acquisition once; unused choices grant nothing.

Rarity is assigned per item in the catalogue. All Mayor sources are recorded as **Ancient**, while their adapted power rarity is **Rare or Legendary**. Ancient labels do not imply one common source power level: opening boons, middle-wave engines and late rule changes are intentionally separated.

All nine identities are preserved. Their old IDs remain stable; an ID containing MY3 is historical and no longer determines its current tier. The catalogue's explicit acquisition and city fields do.

| MY1-02 Impact Liner | Ordinary shared pool | The original +10 maximum/current HP effect is a normal survival pickup. |

| MY2-03 Archive Rack | Ordinary shared pool | Memory growth and a timed part delivery support normal build development. |
| MY3-01 Backflow Ground | Ordinary shared pool | Narrow recoil protection should not consume a highest-tier Mayor choice. |

The seven ordinary entries receive rarities corresponding to their normal relic inspirations where applicable; this can revise their earlier all-Rare/Legendary demonstration labels. These are explicit proposed rarity/distribution changes following the owner's parity instruction, not silent loss of an earlier effect.

## Source, limits and next balance evidence

The [Ancients overview](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Ancients) and individual Ancient pools supply the reference. The [agent study](research/2026-09-18-character-mayor-relics.md) records source differences and adaptation reasoning; the [298-entry inventory](research/2026-09-18-relic-reference.md) preserves stable-versus-beta boundaries. Our gifts use original names, prose and material/recipe mechanics.

Pool checks establish counts, character eligibility, tier separation and enough candidate IDs. A real offer generator must also evaluate current inventory requirements. Combat/economy testing must determine whether each offer is attractive, whether three gifts create excessive compounding supply, and whether City 3 rewards arrive early enough to matter. No such playtest or runtime implementation is claimed here.
