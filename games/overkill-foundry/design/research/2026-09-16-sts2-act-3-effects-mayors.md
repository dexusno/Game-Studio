# Slay the Spire 2 research — Glory, effects and Mayors

Research date and source access: **16 September 2026**. Research contribution for the Overkill Foundry roster; all robot identities, effect translations and tuning suggestions below are **proposals**, not newly selected owner rules or tested balance.

## Evidence and limits

The requested [StS2 main page](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Main) links Glory as Act 3. Direct web opens returned HTTP 403. Search indexing returned substantial individual-page extracts, including moveset tables and update histories; those indexed extracts are the evidence used here, **not verified live full-page reads**. Each source link below was retrieved through that route on the date above. The retrieved crawls ranged from the same day to about three months old. This is a dated wiki snapshot, not proof of the latest installed game build. No gameplay footage or playable StS2 build was tested.

The wiki's Buffs and Debuffs pages explicitly warn that they are under construction. Some enemy pages mix main-branch material, beta warnings and historical values. Observed mechanics are separated from our interpretation. We use paraphrases and original robot designs, not copied descriptions or art. No StS1-only source supports the proposals.

The [Glory act page](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Glory) lists a gentler opening encounter pool followed by a broader normal pool. Its elites are Knight Gang, Mecha Knight and Soul Nexus; its bosses are Queen, Test Subject and Aeonglass. It records Aeonglass replacing Doormaker in v0.107.1. [Doormaker's own page](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Doormaker) labels that enemy deprecated. Do not present Doormaker as part of the current roster.

## City 3 design lesson

**Inference:** late encounters test a developed build in different ways, rather than merely raising HP. A player must choose between focused damage, splitting hits, handling a supporting unit, preparing defence, or ending a fight before scaling becomes unmanageable. Overkill Foundry should combine those pressures with visible enemy intents and its own choice of bullet assembly. A broad inspiration catalogue is useful; it does not mean every below candidate must ship.

Keep the selected rules intact: parts supply all player damage; resource and recipe availability limit actions; only End Turn ends the player turn and activates prepared Shield. No borrowed card-play limit should force End Turn. Shield still resets after the enemy turn, with only explicit permanent upgrades creating exceptions. Whole-number arithmetic applies throughout. Source HP and damage are **reference observations, not proposed Overkill Foundry values**.

**Source qualifications:** Axebot has a beta warning, and its listed Boot Up move is not fully reconciled with the written repeating pattern. Fabricator's displayed High Voltage text says start-of-turn Strength gain, whereas its update history says the tooltip was corrected to end-of-turn; this research therefore does not import that timing. Our adaptation explicitly chooses its own visible timing. StS2 **Plating is not our Armor**: its page describes decaying recurring Block, whereas Klaus selected flat per-hit damage reduction for Armor. Prefer living-source Armor auras to importing enemy Block, so defensive grants remain meaningful during player attacks without creating a Shield-reset exception.

## Effect vocabulary and compatibility

These definitions are proposed vocabulary for the integrator. Existing recipe rows stay untouched until a deliberate content migration. The four current catalogue effects already differ from StS2 and should **not** be silently redefined as percentage effects merely because their names resemble the source.

| Source / existing effect | Proposal | Proposed semantics and compatibility |
|---|---|---|
| Current catalogue **Burn** | **Thermal Runaway N** | Retain the catalogue's draft shield-first damage-over-time behaviour and decay. Preserve recipe-specific triggers. Clarify the player's tick relative to End Turn activation in the eventual unified status specification. |
| Current catalogue **Corrosion**; [StS2 Poison](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Poison) loses HP at turn start and decays | **Acid Etch N** | Retain the catalogue's direct HP loss and one-stack decay, rather than inventing another competing damage-over-time system. Player application is enemy-sourced when an enemy inflicts it; own direct HP costs keep their separate nonlethal rule. |
| Current catalogue **Mark** | **Target Paint N** | Keep flat extra damage on the next qualifying main shot/attack, then consume; do not turn it into Vulnerable. Existing recipe target scope stays authoritative. |
| Current catalogue **Weaken** | **Drive Fault N** | Keep the draft flat reduction from the next attack's total, allocated across its hits in order. This is deliberately different from StS2 Weak. |
| [StS2 Vulnerable](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Vulnerable): increases attack damage received by 50%, with duration stacks | **Breached N turns**, optional later status | Percentage damage amplification is possible with integer rounding, but overlaps Target Paint. Prefer Target Paint in the initial roster; add Breached only if the duration-vs-consumption difference creates a needed role. |
| [StS2 Weak / Frail](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Debuffs): reduce attacks / card Block by 25% for duration | **Signal Damping** / **Barrier Leak** | For initial content, use bounded flat shot suppression or a flat reduction to total prepared Shield when End Turn activates it. No per-part penalty that turns a large multipart shield to zero. These are translations, not exact source mechanics. A living source aura clears on its death. |
| [StS2 Strength](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs): adds attack damage | **Drive N** | Enemy attacks gain N damage per hit, shown in the intent. It adds no player gun base damage. |
| [StS2 Artifact](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs): negates debuff applications | **Packet Filter N** | Each charge blocks one qualifying status application and is consumed; show charges. Define multi-status payload order; this does not negate an entire bullet. |
| [StS2 Thorns](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs): attack-hit retaliation | **Reactive Mesh N** | Return N enemy-sourced damage on each qualifying received hit. This can kill the player under the settled rule; prepared Shield is not active during firing. Show the predicted cost before Fire. |
| [StS2 Vigor](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs): next-attack flat bonus | **Loaded Capacitor N** | Next enemy attack gains N total damage, then consumes the bonus. Player effects remain recipe-defined. |
| [StS2 Buffer](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs): prevents a finite number of HP-loss events | **Impact Fuse N** | A charge prevents HP loss from one direct hit. Does not negate Acid Etch. Small shots spend charges. |
| [StS2 Blur](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Buffs): prevents normal Block expiry | **Reserve Battery**, permanent-upgrade hook only | Only an explicitly described permanent upgrade may preserve Shield across its normal reset. |
| [Devoted Sculptor's Ritual](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Devoted_Sculptor) | **Runaway Clock N** | At that enemy's turn end, gain N Drive. Separate scaling rate from current Drive; both are readable. |
| [Living Shield's Rampart](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Turret_Operator) | **Armor Link N** | While alive, the source grants N Armor to the named ally. Its destruction removes the aura prospectively. This translates source protection without carrying enemy Shield across the selected reset. |
| [Test Subject's Intangible](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Test_Subject) limits damage and HP loss to one | **Phase Screen**, reserve candidate | Avoid near-immunity in the first beta. A visible temporary Armor amount tests timing while allowing all damage builds to make progress. This is an adaptation option, not an assertion of mechanical equivalence. |

**Armor remains Armor:** selected flat reduction on each hit, minimum zero. It neither grants Shield nor retains Shield. Armor, Impact Fuse and Reactive Mesh test different assembly choices; the UI must distinguish them instead of giving all four a generic protection icon.

Effects copied from card games require particular care. Dazed/Slimed/Wound deck pollution, draw prevention, Ethereal hands, card downgrading and Bound have no direct counterpart when all recipes are visible and recipe memory is persistent. Translate their *pressure* into a temporary, visible status or source-linked aura. Do not fill the player's memory with junk, hide recipes, remove all preparation options, add a global energy budget or cap shots. Counterplay must exist through ordinary target selection and bullet/Shield assembly; an optional cleansing recipe should improve the response rather than be the only way to act.

## Mayors and permanent upgrades

**Observed:** the [Ancients overview](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Ancients) describes boons at each act's beginning and separate act pools. [Vakuu](https://slaythespire.wiki.gg/wiki/Slay_the_Spire_2:Vakuu) is a possible Act 3 Ancient and offers one relic from each of three pools. His indexed reward list includes powerful benefits with drawbacks and distinct combat/economy options. Those observations support differentiated opening choices. They do **not** make source unlock conditions, appearance probabilities, mandatory selection, names or individual reward values rules for our game.

**Owner-selected translation, 16 September:** Ancients correspond to **Mayors**, who offer a **rare or legendary permanent upgrade at the beginning of a City**. Preserve the existing choice among three. Mayors are separate from city bosses; do not make defeating the Mayor the prerequisite to receiving a city-start boon.

Possible original Mayor identities to help the main roster designer, all provisional:

- **Mayor Ledger**, a civic accounting robot: choices aimed at resource steering, recipe access and shop economics. No direct copy of a reward that grants all shop stock; our finite-stock and resale economy needs its own cost analysis.
- **Mayor Bastion**, a retired civil-defence chassis: choices for an explicit beginning-of-fight Shield grant, a specified later-turn Shield grant, or max HP increased with equal healing. These fit already selected upgrade categories without adding automatic healing between fights.
- **Mayor Switchboard**, the city's distributed control system: choices for once-per-fight cooling, a precisely bounded recipe interaction, or a first-shot benefit supplied through a clearly described upgrade. Do not substitute card draw or energy; preview the actual recipe/part benefit.

Specific values, three-offer composition, whether Mayor identity varies across runs, and each upgrade's exact timing remain design work. These are content proposals for a reviewable document, not reasons to re-ask settled turn or death rules.

## Recommended material for the integrated roster

Prioritise **Aegis Tug / Beltgun Porter**, **Municipal Assembly Rig**, **Overclock Preacher**, **Wiretick Pack**, **Vault Ram**, **Grid Conductor**, **Citadel Breacher**, and one City 3 boss initially. They cover protection support, target priority, scaling, multiple targets, combined-shot advantage, alternating attack shapes, finite debuff protection and a climax with one clear pressure. Keep the Siphon pair and Compliance Triad as related alternatives rather than doubling the same suppression puzzle unnecessarily.

No Overkill Foundry combat statistics have been validated here. The first balance pass must use our actual haul, cooldowns, starting recipe sets, Armor and Shield timing. Count shots, part costs, expected HP loss, recipe availability and dead turns. In particular, check that Armor/recoil enemies do not dominate every encounter, that many-small-shot builds get favourable encounters too, and that summon encounters cannot provide unlimited loot. Source behaviour alone is not evidence of enjoyable or fair balance in our resource system.
