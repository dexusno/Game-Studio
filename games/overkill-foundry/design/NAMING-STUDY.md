# Naming study — 14 September 2026

**Recommendation: Overkill Foundry.** It joins the recurring decision—crafting—with the desired payoff of a spectacular assembled shot. **Scrap Overlords** is the strongest playful alternative; **Salvage Rebellion** is the strongest resistance-theme alternative. **Owner decision after this review: Overkill Foundry selected**, 14 September 2026. The directory/catalogue ID is now `overkill-foundry`. Other candidates below remain unselected alternatives.

The owner requested a commercially appealing name informed by popular/trending games, with checks for prior use. The evidence below supports a positioning judgment and a bounded collision screen. It does not establish that naming caused another game's success, that a candidate will sell, or that any phrase is globally unused or legally cleared. All sources and search results were accessed **14 September 2026**.

## Why this direction

The defining promise is making scrap into powerful combinations while planning around enemy intent. The claw is now a supporting system with an optional once-per-fight precision attempt, so a claw-led title would emphasize the wrong part of the experience. The proposed store descriptor is:

> A turn-based roguelike about forging scrap into absurd firepower.

This is proposed positioning, not a change to the game's rules or a claim that the power payoff has been tested. Recipes are always accessible in memory, so calling this a deckbuilder would create the wrong expectation.

Several popular adjacent games use compact names and explain their combination systems in nearby store copy. Our inference is to give this game one memorable promise rather than squeeze its entire setting and rules into the title. These examples were selected for audience and interaction relevance, not to represent a statistically sampled market.

## Shortlist and objections

| Priority | Candidate | Appeal | Main objection / observed nearby use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Overkill Foundry** | Communicates making excessive firepower; ordinary spelling and a clear connection to the assembled-shot payoff. | Could imply a factory sim or real-time shooter. Both [FOUNDRY](https://store.steampowered.com/app/983870/FOUNDRY/) and [OVERKILL Software](https://www.starbreeze.com/overkill/) exist. Strong positioning but crowded component words. |
| 2 | **Scrap Overlords** | Short, energetic, concrete material plus a power fantasy. | The player is a mercenary, so “overlords” may imply commanding an army or playing the enemy. [Chaos Overlords](https://www.gog.com/en/game/chaos_overlords) is an existing title; [Scrap Age: Survivors](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3143100/Scrap_Age_Survivors/) occupies nearby robot/scrap combat wording. |
| 3 | **Salvage Rebellion** | Connects reclaimed materials to human resistance. | Less immediate combat payoff; could imply exploration or a narrative campaign. Rebellion is an existing developer/publisher, visible on [Rogue Trooper Redux](https://store.steampowered.com/app/529490/Rogue_Trooper_Redux/). |
| Reserve | **Foundry Renegades** | Crafting plus a roster of independent fighters. | Longer; “renegades” suggests a character history not established in the design. FOUNDRY is existing; [Wargames Foundry](https://www.wargamesfoundry.com/products/sb034-afro-caribbean-renegades) also uses nearby tabletop-product wording. |
| Reserve | **Revolt Foundry** | Compact industrial resistance. | Less natural English, unclear relationship between the words, same factory-sim expectation. |
| Drop from shortlist | **Core Rebellion** | Broad technology/resistance identity. | Cores are secondary sellable loot, so the title says little about the interaction. The exact phrase is already a [Redbubble merchandise title](https://www.redbubble.com/i/t-shirt/Core-Rebellion-by-HQ-stickers/172322733/lrcw); the reversed phrase is the [Rebellion Core](https://docs.splinterlands.com/cards/sets-editions/rebellion/rebellion-core) Splinterlands set label. The latter was verified in indexed official documentation; direct fetch was unsupported. |

The market specialist preferred Overkill Foundry; the collision-screen specialist preferred Scrap Overlords because of component-word crowding. The integrated recommendation retains Overkill Foundry for its fit to the actual loop, with that objection visible. Neither judgment is a legal risk rating.

One additional broad-search neighbor is [Found-Ry by OverKill Hill](https://overkillhill.com/projects/found-ry/), a browser workbench for creating Custom GPTs. It is not an exact game-title match, but adds software/search crowding around the same component words.

## Completed conflict searches

The six complete phrases above were individually searched in quotation marks on the general web, with `game` appended, and with storefront domain filters covering **Steam, itch.io, GOG, Epic, Apple App Store and Google Play**. Follow-up queries separated itch, GOG/Epic and Apple/Google to reduce Steam dominance. No exact game/app title was identified in returned results. Engines sometimes relaxed quotes and matched descriptions; those were not treated as title matches. This is web-index coverage, not a complete regional catalogue export.

Actual close-variant queries were `"Scrap Overlord" game`, `"Foundry Revolt" game`, `"Salvage Rebels" game`, `"Rebellion Core" game`, `"Overkill" "Foundry" game title`, and `"Foundry Renegade" game`. They surfaced the nearby uses above. No exhaustive spelling, translation or phonetic search was performed.

Native Steam searches additionally inspected all displayed titles for Scrap Overlords (18 visible matches; one excluded by preferences remained uninspected), Salvage Rebellion (11) and Revolt Foundry (one: Goblin Shaman). None was the exact candidate. The other three had indexed-store coverage only. Native search matches descriptions as well as titles, so the result counts are not counts of competing names. Reproduction: [Scrap Overlords search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=Scrap%20Overlords), [Salvage Rebellion search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=Salvage%20Rebellion), [Revolt Foundry search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=Revolt%20Foundry). These are search logs, not certificates of absence.

No domain, handle or trademark was registered and no public announcement was made. The owner selected the working title after reviewing this screen. This screen does not cover unpublished uses, company-name registries, US state marks, every national registry, translated/sound-alike marks, logos or all unregistered commercial use. Exact-phrase absence alone does not resolve similarity conflicts. Recheck the chosen title and obtain any needed clearance before a public commitment; do not describe these names as guaranteed unused.

## Next decision

The owner selected **Overkill Foundry** through an explicit reply after the recommendation. Naming is complete for this milestone; resume the design questions in the handoff. For a later audience check, show the same art and turn-based descriptor under the top three names to 8–12 relevant players in rotated order; ask what they expect to do and whether they can recall/type the name. A proposed rejection signal is more than one-third still expecting a factory sim, real-time shooter or army-control game. That would be a small comprehension test, not a purchase forecast. No outreach or test has been conducted.
