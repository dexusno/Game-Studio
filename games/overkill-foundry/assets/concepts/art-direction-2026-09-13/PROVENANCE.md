# Art direction boards — provenance

Generated 2026-09-13 for the owner's broad art-direction exploration using the built-in ImageGen tool. The tool does not expose a model-version identifier in its result; no exact model version is claimed. No CLI/API fallback was used and no third-party artwork was supplied as an input image.

Exact prompts, family names, variants, asset paths, hashes and static observations are recorded in [the board register](../../../design/art-direction-boards.json). The review and production assessment are in [the art-direction atlas](../../../design/ART-DIRECTION.md).

The original generated PNGs are copied non-destructively into `raw/` and excluded from Git. Their machine-local import records are also ignored. Committed WebP files use lossless encoding at the original dimensions, and decoded RGBA pixels are compared for equality with the PNGs. No content changes, crops or resizing were performed by the conversion script. The generated images were visually inspected in tool output, which establishes only static concept coverage.

These are concept alternatives, not approved runtime assets, functional UI, selected characters or a final story. The output is AI-generated under the platform's applicable terms; it is not assigned a third-party stock licence. Final production use remains subject to ordinary asset selection and originality review. No competitor art, logo or source code was imported. Studio distribution licence remains unset.
