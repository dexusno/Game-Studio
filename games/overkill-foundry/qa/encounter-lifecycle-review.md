## Reproduction and limits

[Commands and artifacts](encounter-lifecycle/README.md) rebuild the immutable
copies and run the independent probes. [Evidence](encounter-lifecycle-evidence.json)
records executable/library/log/source identities and the separate supplemental
source references. No production, author-test, support-ledger, version, STATUS or
Unreal file was edited.

The evidence uses prepared route prefixes, resources, statuses, active protection,
two controlled mixed-robot boundaries and terminal ammunition. It tests mechanics,
not natural acquisition or earned balance. It supplies no all-encounter guarantee,
full-profile claim, disk durability, graphical inspection, timing/input/audio or
human-play evidence. Separate recipe, physical-type, route and profile reviews
remain separate evidence. Two corrected QA fixture mistakes are recorded locally;
they did not require production changes.
