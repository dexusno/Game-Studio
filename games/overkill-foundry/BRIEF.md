# Overkill Foundry — independent game brief

Owner-selected title: **Overkill Foundry**, 14 September 2026. Catalogue ID: `overkill-foundry`. Owner-separated from Magnet Sweep on 14 September 2026; fresh gameplay implementation, currently concept stage. Planned platform/tool direction: Windows and Unreal. Development timeline is unconstrained; implementation scope and numerical budgets are not approved.

## Player promise

Build a devastating shot and enough defence from the scrap you have, read the robots' intentions, and turn victories into a stronger mercenary weapon system. Travel between fortified cities in a dystopian world taken over by a hostile AI. **A full campaign contains 3 cities initially, subject to beta balancing.** Each city has distinct surroundings and a wave of **10–20 encounters**, including non-combat Mystery visits and the final boss. Each completed Mystery counts once toward city progress; ordinary shop revisits do not count. The final boss is a single encounter option that replaces the usual three choices. Clearing the third city completes the campaign route; finale presentation and rewards remain open.

## Selected loop

Choose a save profile and an unlocked mercenary. Each profile holds at most one ongoing campaign; separate profiles have independent campaigns and persistent discovery/unlock records. Mara is initially available; clearing a city with Mara unlocks Ivo, with Ivo unlocks Ada, and with Ada unlocks Noor for that profile. Each mercenary starts with a basic weapon and core recipes. At city arrival choose from three weapon upgrades. For ordinary encounter selection before the city boss, choose among three semi-random encounters with at least one Regular; Officer and Mystery each appear about one quarter of the time among offers. Their exact joint distribution remains open. **Continue after quitting during an unfinished fight restarts it from the beginning with the same seed and original fight-entry state.** Entry HP and supplies are restored, and repeating the same choices reproduces random results; the player may try different choices. Death still ends the campaign. If that profile already has an unfinished campaign, Start New Game requires confirmation before replacing it; cancellation keeps it intact. Collection history and character unlocks persist. Between-fight resume details remain open.

Inspect enemies' intent, strength and buffs, then OK. Choose baseline ingredients and Collect with the rear claw, or spend the one optional Precision attempt available this fight. A very short collection/furnace animation delivers resources. In the loadout, open full-screen Recipe, focus a recipe and Use it with available materials: gun/Shield recipes craft parts, while Utility recipes activate directly and produce no part; greyed recipes remain inspectable. Shop is also accessible during preparation. Its stock stays the same throughout the fight; reopening it or advancing rounds does not refresh offers or reset purchases. It restocks after each completed fight when the campaign continues; offers have limited quantities, purchases reduce stock, and sold-out offers stay unavailable until restocking. Exact stock counts and the detailed offer-refresh method remain to define. The shop also buys unused crafted parts for half their allocated ingredient value at its current resource prices, rounded down to whole credits per part. A craft producing several identical parts splits its ingredient value equally among them before applying the sale factor and rounding. Copied parts retain the original's allocated ingredient basis and sell for the same amount under the same current shop prices. Selling removes the part, while its recipe remains in memory. Raw materials cannot be sold to the shop; buying them remains available. Parts and raw materials are separate pools with no reserve storage cap within a fight; unused stock carries between rounds and clears at fight end. **Crafting and firing have no separate global energy/action-point budget:** materials, available parts and recipe use/cooldown requirements provide the action limits. Individual bullets and shields have no fixed part-count cap: build size is limited only by resources and available parts under the existing recipe rules. Exact costs and supply amounts remain to tune; the 20-recipe memory limit remains separate. The [costed beta proposal](design/BALANCE-RESEARCH.md#current-beta-haul-recommendation--15-september-2026) recommends 10 materials per ordinary haul (8 baseline + 2 steered); these numerical values are not yet owner-selected.

Select parts for bullet and shield. **The gun has zero base damage: all bullet damage and effects come from the parts assembled into it.** Each recipe specifies which targets receive its status effects; multi-target shots have no blanket main-target-only rule. Load requires a nonempty bullet, then select permitted targets and Fire. Before firing, the player may unload and change selected parts. Recipe and Shop remain usable while loaded: craft available recipes, use Utilities or buy goods under their normal requirements; closing returns to the loaded preparation state. Fire consumes bullet parts, leaves prepared Shield parts pending, shows brief firing/impact feedback and returns to preparation while combat continues. Only End Turn activates the prepared shield; crafting, placing parts, Load and Fire do not. Once activated, protection lasts through the enemy turn, with the existing reset/upgrade rules applied at enemy-turn end. Craft, load and fire further shots while parts/resources and recipe availability allow. Only **End Turn** ends the player turn and sends surviving enemies through their visible intents; it activates the prepared shield before enemy actions regardless of shot count, without ammunition or Load and without firing a shot. Shield absorbs ordinary damage before HP. Clear round Shield/builds and reduce eligible active cooldowns by one at enemy-turn end, skipping counters applied by a recipe used that round. Shots do not trigger those resets or another collection. Repeat until enemies are killed/fled or the player dies. Robots announce a planned escape 4 turns in advance and continue their normal attacks and abilities during the warning; show both their current action intent and escape countdown. Fleeing robots drop no cores or recipe loot; killed enemies remain eligible for their existing loot. If every enemy escapes without any kills, the fight yields no cores or recipe rewards. **Death ends the current campaign and requires a new game**, with the selected character/basic-weapon/starter-recipe flow. Game Over shows the gloating evil AI. The active profile's Collection keeps discovered recipe history after death and across campaigns, separately from active recipe memory. Seeing a recipe offered records it in Collection even if skipped; accepting it into memory remains separate. Recipes have no permanent unlock requirements: all non-starter recipes can appear during the first campaign under normal character, encounter, rarity and city/level offer rules. Character unlocks persist across campaigns in the selected order Mara → Ivo → Ada → Noor; other profile progression remains open. Exclusive recipe pools follow the chosen unlocked mercenary without additional recipe unlock requirements.

**Confirmed continuation rules:** reversible Load, multiple shots and exclusive End Turn supersede the earlier single-shot turn. Only End Turn activates the prepared shield, including parts added after the last shot. Prepared values add at that activation: 8 + 5 = 13 with no other changes. Exact used-part accounting, secondary-effect ordering and other non-Ammo timing still need definition; the draft gun damage needs review under this flow. Recipe/Shop access while loaded is selected. Crafting refunds, resolved-effect undo and remaining loaded-bullet reservation/validation are not selected by allowing unloading.

## Build and persistence

An otherwise unspecified Utility damage bonus applies to the next shot only. Bonuses lasting the rest of the turn or fight must say so explicitly. Unused next-shot Utility damage bonuses expire at End Turn and do not carry over.

Recipe memory starts with a hard capacity of **20 recipes**, expandable by permanent upgrades and subject to balancing after beta testing; all stored recipes are visible without a deck/draw system. Duplicate copies of the exact same recipe are allowed, each occupying one memory slot; each copy tracks its own cooldown and once-per-turn use, so using one does not lock another. Every use still pays its normal costs. Start with **12 recipes: 8 shared and 4 character-specific**, leaving 8 free slots. Both the starter set and capacity are provisional values to balance through beta runs; individual recipe tuning remains draft. **Every new fight starts with all recipe cooldown counters cleared.** Numeric cooldowns start at recipe Use, whether producing a part or activating a Utility effect. A cooldown of N blocks the next N rounds naturally; automatic enemy-turn-end ticks skip the use round. Cooldown 1 used in round 4 is blocked round 5 and ready round 6; cooldown 2 is ready round 7. Explicit cooling can make either ready sooner; global cooling removes X from every cooling recipe. At zero counters, an affordable recipe can be used again immediately. No-cooldown recipes are once per turn unless a permanent upgrade says otherwise. Unused materials and parts carry between rounds but both clear at fight end. Recipes/upgrades persist for the current game. **Remaining player HP carries into the next fight; a fight transition does not automatically heal.** Explicit healing can change HP; healing availability and tuning remain to define. Shield resets after the complete enemy turn by default, unless a permanent upgrade explicitly says otherwise. Permanent upgrades may grant beginning Shield each turn, at fight start or on a specified turn; fresh grants do not automatically preserve old Shield. Recipe drafts do not supply reset exceptions. Upgrades may also grant fresh supplies on their defined triggers.

Accept desired loot items individually and Skip the remainder. Recipe rewards allow zero or one of three; at full memory, exchange a held recipe or skip without changing it. Cores sell for credits. Permanent claw upgrades come from shops or Officer loot and activate on purchase/acceptance. The 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Neither implicitly grants another Precision attempt.

Shops offer no direct healing service. They may sell healing recipes or permanent upgrades that increase maximum HP and heal for that same amount. For example, gaining +10 maximum HP changes 40/80 HP to 50/90. Buying a recipe does not itself activate its healing effect. Stock, prices and upgrade amounts remain to balance.

## Content and presentation

The catalogue has 606 drafts: 126 shared and 120 exclusive to each of four proposed mercenaries, for 246 possible recipes per character. Character identities, starter sizes, recipe numbers and detailed status rules remain proposals where the owner has not selected them.

Use the owner-supplied [style anchor](design/art%20anchor.png). Prepare in 16B's 2.5D side view with claw behind the gun and enemy intent ahead; use 16C's 3D perspective for action. Each bullet and shield interface must support at least 20 applied parts and scale to larger resource-permitted builds; there is no fixed part-count cap. Physical-parts versus combined-effects presentation remains undecided. Illustrations and the old camera motion study are references only.

## Next proof and limits

Reconcile Utility catalogue dependencies, then resolve remaining build-effect activation, shot-versus-round effects and Load controls, supply amounts, enemy moves, shop stock/prices, detailed Mystery outcomes, exact escape countdown/departure ordering, loot amounts and offer tuning, other profile persistence and save/Game Over controls. Reconcile conflicting Shield recipes with the settled upgrade-only exceptions; do not reopen the base rule. Global cooling creates balance overlaps that need review. A proposed first demo is one mercenary, one city environment, about 30 recipes and a three-fight sample; this has not been selected and does not replace the full city wave.

The first playable should test whether different enemy intentions and supplies cause meaningful changes in crafting, saving, attack and defence, and whether an earned reward changes a later choice. Game feel and commercial appeal are untested. Naming research will screen current market relevance and public conflicts without treating an absence of search hits as legal clearance.
