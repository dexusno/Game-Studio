# Overkill Foundry — independent game brief

**Permanent upgrades, selected direction 18 September:** campaign upgrades fill the role of STS2 relics, including high-tech tools that may explicitly change game defaults. Shared and mercenary-specific pools are defined in the [upgrade catalogue](design/UPGRADE-CATALOGUE.md). Common/Uncommon/Rare source tiers map directly; special source pools have stated rarity mappings. Mayor gifts draw specifically from Ancient relics and become stronger over the three city waves. Individual effects, values and distributions remain proposals; existing innate character abilities are preserved.

Owner-selected title: **Overkill Foundry**, 14 September 2026. Catalogue ID: `overkill-foundry`. Owner-separated from Magnet Sweep on 14 September 2026; fresh gameplay implementation, currently concept stage. Planned platform/tool direction: Windows and Unreal. Development timeline is unconstrained; implementation scope and numerical budgets are not approved.

## Player promise

Build a devastating shot and enough defence from the scrap you have, read the robots' intentions, and turn victories into a stronger mercenary weapon system. Travel between fortified cities in a dystopian world taken over by a hostile AI. **Each mercenary has a different progression tree and its own three cities: four distinct campaigns and twelve city identities in total. A played campaign contains 3 cities initially, subject to beta balancing.** Robot attacks are procedurally generated from gated pools: earlier cities/fights draw from a smaller pool of lesser robots, with harder opponents and combinations becoming eligible later. The [progression proposal](design/CAMPAIGN-PROGRESSION.md) specifies draft routes and placements; randomness cannot bypass the gates. Each city has distinct surroundings and a wave of **10–20 encounters**, including non-combat Mystery visits and the final boss. Each completed Mystery counts once toward city progress; ordinary shop revisits do not count. The final boss is a single encounter option that replaces the usual three choices. Clearing the third city completes the campaign route; finale presentation and rewards remain open.

## Selected loop

Choose a save profile and an unlocked mercenary. Each profile holds at most one ongoing campaign; separate profiles have independent campaigns and persistent discovery/unlock records. Mara is initially available; clearing a city with Mara unlocks Ivo, with Ivo unlocks Ada, and with Ada unlocks Noor for that profile. Each mercenary starts with a basic weapon and core recipes. At each city arrival the Mayor offers a choice among three Rare or Legendary permanent upgrades, serving the Ancient-like role selected by the owner. For ordinary encounter selection before the city boss, choose among three semi-random encounters with at least one Regular; Officer and Mystery each appear about one quarter of the time among offers. Their exact joint distribution remains open. **Continue after quitting during an unfinished fight restarts it from the beginning with the same seed and original fight-entry state.** Entry HP and supplies are restored, and repeating the same choices reproduces random results; the player may try different choices. Death still ends the campaign. If that profile already has an unfinished campaign, Start New Game requires confirmation before replacing it; cancellation keeps it intact. Collection history and character unlocks persist. Between-fight resume details remain open.

Inspect enemies' intent, strength and buffs, then OK. Choose baseline ingredients and Collect with the rear claw, or spend the one optional Precision attempt available this fight. A very short collection/furnace animation delivers resources. In the loadout, open full-screen Recipe, focus a recipe and Use it with available materials: gun/Shield recipes craft parts, while Utility recipes activate directly and produce no part; greyed recipes remain inspectable. Shop is also accessible during preparation. Its stock stays the same throughout the fight; reopening it or advancing rounds does not refresh offers or reset purchases. It restocks after each completed fight when the campaign continues; offers have limited quantities, purchases reduce stock, and sold-out offers stay unavailable until restocking. Exact stock counts and the detailed offer-refresh method remain to define. The shop also buys unused crafted parts for half the normal ingredient value of producing one through that part's main recipe, using current shop resource prices and rounding down to whole credits. Rare-recipe rebates, discounts, alternative production, copied parts and batches do not change that part's reference cost. Each different output uses its own main-recipe value; do not split the actual batch's costs for resale. Selling removes the part, while its recipe remains in memory. Raw materials cannot be sold to the shop; buying them remains available. Parts and raw materials are separate pools with no reserve storage cap within a fight; unused stock carries between rounds and clears at fight end. **Crafting and firing have no separate global energy/action-point budget:** materials, available parts and recipe use/cooldown requirements provide the action limits. Individual bullets and shields have no fixed part-count cap: build size is limited only by resources and available parts under the existing recipe rules. Exact costs and supply amounts remain to tune; the 20-recipe memory limit remains separate. The [costed beta proposal](design/BALANCE-RESEARCH.md#current-beta-haul-recommendation--15-september-2026) recommends 10 materials per ordinary haul (8 baseline + 2 steered); these numerical values are not yet owner-selected.

Select parts for bullet and shield. **The gun has zero ordinary base damage.** Parts supply the shot; explicit character effects such as Hot Barrel and Charged Barrel can modify it. A general rule does not silently cancel such a specific exception. Eligible percentage damage bonuses from different parts add together by default before the combined bonus is applied: +20% and +30% give +50%. Preserve explicit recipe conditions and non-stacking restrictions. This does not change bonus duration, effect targeting or the number of effect triggers. All damage is stored, previewed and resolved as whole numbers. The rework retains percentage bonuses and calculates their whole-number results using integer division, discarding remainders without carrying them into another hit or turn. Add eligible percentage bonuses before calculating the combined whole damage bonus once. For example, 9 damage with +60% gains 5 whole damage and resolves as 14. Split hits and damage reductions also produce whole damage; no fractional remainder is retained. Each recipe specifies which targets receive its status effects; multi-target shots have no blanket main-target-only rule. Different eligible spreading parts aimed at the same extra robot add their whole damage contributions: 10 + 8 gives 18 before defence. Explicit non-stacking restrictions still apply. Each eligible spreading part produces a separate hit: 10 and 8 are two hits. Per-hit defences and reactions are evaluated for each qualifying hit under their own conditions. Those spread hits resolve in the order their contributing parts were placed in the bullet. A later spread hit is lost if an earlier hit from the same bullet has already killed its target. It deals no damage, causes no hit-based reaction and is not automatically redirected; fired parts remain consumed. Load requires a nonempty bullet, then select permitted targets and Fire. Before firing, the player may unload and change selected parts. Recipe and Shop remain usable while loaded: craft available recipes, use Utilities or buy goods under their normal requirements; closing returns to the loaded preparation state. Fire consumes bullet parts, leaves installed Shield parts in place, shows brief firing/impact feedback and returns to preparation while combat continues. Installed Shield parts protect automatically when enemies attack; crafting, placing parts, Load and Fire do not. Once activated, protection lasts through the enemy turn, with the existing reset/upgrade rules applied at enemy-turn end. Craft, load and fire further shots while parts/resources and recipe availability allow. Only **End Turn** ends the player turn and sends surviving enemies through their visible intents; installed Shield parts protect automatically when enemies attack, without ammunition or bullet Load. Shield absorbs ordinary damage before HP. Clear round Shield/builds and reduce eligible active cooldowns by one at enemy-turn end, skipping counters applied by a recipe used that round. Shots do not trigger those resets or another collection. Repeat until enemies are killed/fled or the player dies. Once player death is resolved, remaining hit resolution stops and Game Over follows under the existing death rule. Robots announce a planned escape 4 turns in advance and continue their normal attacks and abilities while the warning counts down, until the departure turn; show both their current action intent and escape countdown. On the departure turn, escaping is the robot's action and replaces its normal attack or ability; show Escape as that turn's intent. It does not take another normal action before fleeing. Fleeing robots drop no cores or recipe loot; killed enemies remain eligible for their existing loot. If every enemy escapes without any kills, the fight yields no cores or recipe rewards. **Death ends the current campaign and requires a new game**, with the selected character/basic-weapon/starter-recipe flow. Game Over shows the gloating evil AI. If the player and the last robot die in the same resolution, the outcome is Game Over. Direct HP costs or self-inflicted HP loss from effects the player uses must leave at least 1 HP. A recipe with player HP cost C requires at least C + 1 current HP to use. Pay the full HP cost; no partial payment is allowed. A 3-HP cost is unavailable at 3 HP or less; at 4 HP it leaves 1 HP. Printed recipe costs remain draft balance values. Damage returned by a robot's recoil buff is enemy damage, even when the player's attack triggers it, and can kill the player if it reduces HP to 0. The nonlethal rule for the player's own effects does not prevent robot recoil or other enemy damage. The active profile's Collection keeps discovered recipe history after death and across campaigns, separately from active recipe memory. Seeing a recipe offered records it in Collection even if skipped; accepting it into memory remains separate. Recipes have no permanent unlock requirements: all non-starter recipes can appear during the first campaign under normal character, encounter, rarity and city/level offer rules. Character unlocks persist across campaigns in the selected order Mara → Ivo → Ada → Noor; other profile progression remains open. Exclusive recipe pools follow the chosen unlocked mercenary without additional recipe unlock requirements.

**Confirmed continuation rules:** reversible Load, multiple shots and exclusive End Turn supersede the earlier single-shot turn. Installed Shield parts protect automatically when enemies attack, including parts added after the last shot. Installed values add for protection against enemy attacks: 8 + 5 = 13 with no other changes. Exact used-part accounting, secondary-effect ordering and other non-Ammo timing still need definition; the draft gun damage needs review under this flow. Recipe/Shop access while loaded is selected. End Turn unloads any unfired bullet and returns its unused parts to the available part pool; no assembled, loaded bullet carries into the next player turn. This does not fire or consume those parts, refund crafting materials, or preserve next-shot bonuses that expire at End Turn. Parts in a loaded bullet cannot be sold until that bullet is unloaded. Other eligible unused parts remain saleable while the bullet is loaded. Crafting refunds, resolved-effect undo and remaining loaded-bullet reservation/validation are not selected by allowing unloading.

## Build and persistence

An otherwise unspecified Utility damage bonus applies to the next shot only. Bonuses lasting the rest of the turn or fight must say so explicitly. Unused next-shot Utility damage bonuses expire at End Turn and do not carry over.

**New Game begins a new campaign across the three-city route. Credits reset to the starting balance on New Game**, with that starting amount still to balance. Credits carry between fights and cities within the same campaign. Profile Collection history and character unlocks retain their selected persistence. New Game also clears all unsold energy cores from the previous campaign; cores carry between fights and cities in the current campaign until sold.

Recipe memory starts with a hard capacity of **20 recipes**, expandable by permanent upgrades and subject to balancing after beta testing; all stored recipes are visible without a deck/draw system. Duplicate copies of the exact same recipe are allowed, each occupying one memory slot; each copy tracks its own cooldown and once-per-turn use, so using one does not lock another. Every use still pays its normal costs. Start with **12 recipes: 8 shared and 4 character-specific**, leaving 8 free slots. Both the starter set and capacity are provisional values to balance through beta runs; individual recipe tuning remains draft. **Every new fight starts with all recipe cooldown counters cleared.** Numeric cooldowns start at recipe Use, whether producing a part or activating a Utility effect. A cooldown of N blocks the next N rounds naturally; automatic enemy-turn-end ticks skip the use round. Cooldown 1 used in round 4 is blocked round 5 and ready round 6; cooldown 2 is ready round 7. Explicit cooling can make either ready sooner; global cooling removes X from every cooling recipe. At zero counters, an affordable recipe can be used again immediately. No-cooldown recipes are once per turn unless a permanent upgrade says otherwise. Unused materials and parts carry between rounds but both clear at fight end. Recipes/upgrades persist for the current game. **Remaining player HP carries into the next fight; a fight transition does not automatically heal.** Explicit healing can change HP; healing availability and tuning remain to define. Shield resets after the complete enemy turn by default, unless a permanent upgrade explicitly says otherwise. Permanent upgrades may grant beginning Shield each turn, at fight start or on a specified turn; fresh grants do not automatically preserve old Shield. Recipe drafts do not supply reset exceptions. Upgrades may also grant fresh supplies on their defined triggers.

Accept desired loot items individually and Skip the remainder. Recipe rewards allow zero or one of three; at full memory, exchange a held recipe or skip without changing it. Cores sell for credits. Permanent claw upgrades come from shops or Officer loot and activate on purchase/acceptance. The 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. A pending temporary next-round gathering boost expires if the fight ends before it triggers; it does not carry into the next fight. This clears the pending boost, not the recipe from memory or retained permanent claw upgrades. Neither implicitly grants another Precision attempt.

Shops offer no direct healing service. They may sell healing recipes or permanent upgrades that increase maximum HP and heal for that same amount. For example, gaining +10 maximum HP changes 40/80 HP to 50/90. Buying a recipe does not itself activate its healing effect. Stock, prices and upgrade amounts remain to balance.

## Content and presentation

**Inherent abilities requested, 16 September:** each mercenary has a unique built-in passive. The [first proposal](design/MERCENARY-ABILITIES.md) gives Mara Heat-spend recovery, Ivo follow-up targeting after Acid Etch, Ada Bolt repair after a paid Helper attack, and Noor Charge after effective Utility cooling. These require no memory slot or purchase. Names and values remain draft; the obsolete independent gun-damage traits are replaced without changing recipe rows.

A [three-city robot roster](design/ENEMY-ROSTER.md), [effect glossary](design/ROBOT-EFFECTS.md) and [Mayor upgrade draft](design/MAYORS.md) were developed through the owner-authorized two-agent STS2 wiki study on 16 September. They propose 42 encounter entries across the three cities, with one final boss chosen from each city's alternatives. City identities, new effects and numerical values remain proposals; only the Mayor role/rarity and previously settled rules are owner-selected. These are documented designs, not a playable build or verified balance.

The catalogue has 606 drafts: 126 shared and 120 exclusive to each of four proposed mercenaries, for 246 possible recipes per character. Character identities, starter sizes, recipe numbers and detailed status rules remain proposals where the owner has not selected them.

Use the owner-supplied [style anchor](design/art%20anchor.png). Prepare in 16B's 2.5D side view with claw behind the gun and enemy intent ahead; use 16C's 3D perspective for action. Each bullet and shield interface must support at least 20 applied parts and scale to larger resource-permitted builds; there is no fixed part-count cap. Physical-parts versus combined-effects presentation remains undecided. Illustrations and the old camera motion study are references only.

## Next proof and limits

**Recipe audit updated through joint review, 18 September:** [the report](design/RECIPE-RULE-AUDIT.md) covers all 606 recipes with 0 confirmed conflicts and 111 clarification cases. The owner confirms that explicit character effects can override base rules. Review found Hot Barrel and Charged Barrel wrongly invalidated; both are restored with their original draft values, and all thirteen recipe interactions are preserved. Eight cleanup edits are reversed; all 606 recipe rows match their pre-cleanup text. Ivo's Find the Seam and Ada's Bolt feature were retained. Quench Recovery and Residual Current remain unselected alternatives, not replacements or automatic extra traits. Review remaining timing/wording questions without overriding specific exceptions; no runtime or balance claim follows.

Reconcile Utility catalogue dependencies, then resolve remaining build-effect activation, shot-versus-round effects and Load controls, supply amounts, enemy moves, shop stock/prices, detailed Mystery outcomes, exact escape countdown display/tick conventions, loot amounts and offer tuning, other profile persistence and save/Game Over controls. Reconcile conflicting Shield recipes with the settled upgrade-only exceptions; do not reopen the base rule. Global cooling creates balance overlaps that need review. A proposed first demo is one mercenary, one city environment, about 30 recipes and a three-fight sample; this has not been selected and does not replace the full city wave.

The first playable should test whether different enemy intentions and supplies cause meaningful changes in crafting, saving, attack and defence, and whether an earned reward changes a later choice. Game feel and commercial appeal are untested. Naming research will screen current market relevance and public conflicts without treating an absence of search hits as legal clearance.
