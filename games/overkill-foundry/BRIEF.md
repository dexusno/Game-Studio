# Overkill Foundry — independent game brief

Owner-selected title: **Overkill Foundry**, 14 September 2026. Catalogue ID: `overkill-foundry`. Owner-separated from Magnet Sweep on 14 September 2026; fresh gameplay implementation, currently concept stage. Planned platform/tool direction: Windows and Unreal. Development timeline is unconstrained; implementation scope and numerical budgets are not approved.

## Player promise

Build a devastating shot and enough defence from the scrap you have, read the robots' intentions, and turn victories into a stronger mercenary weapon system. Travel between fortified cities in a dystopian world taken over by a hostile AI. Each city has distinct surroundings and a wave of 10–20 fights, ending in a boss.

## Selected loop

Choose a save profile and an unlocked mercenary. Mara is initially available; clearing a city with Mara unlocks Ivo, with Ivo unlocks Ada, and with Ada unlocks Noor for that profile. Each mercenary starts with a basic weapon and core recipes. At city arrival choose from three weapon upgrades. Between fights choose among three semi-random encounters with at least one Regular; Officer and Mystery each appear about one quarter of the time among offers. Their exact joint distribution remains open. **Continue after quitting during an unfinished fight restarts it from the beginning with the same seed and original fight-entry state.** Entry HP and supplies are restored, and repeating the same choices reproduces random results; the player may try different choices. Death still ends the campaign. Campaign save-slot limits and between-fight resume details remain open.

Inspect enemies' intent, strength and buffs, then OK. Choose baseline ingredients and Collect with the rear claw, or spend the one optional Precision attempt available this fight. A very short collection/furnace animation delivers resources. In the loadout, open full-screen Recipe, focus a recipe and Use it with available materials: gun/Shield recipes craft parts, while Utility recipes activate directly and produce no part; greyed recipes remain inspectable. Shop is also accessible during preparation. Parts and raw materials are separate pools.

Select parts for bullet and shield. Load requires a nonempty bullet, then select permitted targets and Fire. Before firing, the player may unload and change selected parts. Fire consumes the shot parts, shows brief firing/impact feedback and returns to preparation while combat continues. Craft, load and fire further shots while parts/resources and recipe availability allow. Only **End Turn** ends the player turn and sends surviving enemies through their visible intents; it also permits zero-shot rounds. Shield absorbs ordinary damage before HP. Clear round Shield/builds and reduce eligible active cooldowns by one at enemy-turn end, skipping counters applied by a recipe used that round. Shots do not trigger those resets or another collection. Repeat until enemies are killed/fled or the player dies. **Death ends the current campaign and requires a new game**, with the selected character/basic-weapon/starter-recipe flow. Game Over shows the gloating evil AI. The active profile's Collection keeps discovered recipe history after death and across campaigns, separately from active recipe memory. Seeing a recipe offered records it in Collection even if skipped; accepting it into memory remains separate. Recipes have no permanent unlock requirements: all non-starter recipes can appear during the first campaign under normal character, encounter, rarity and city/level offer rules. Character unlocks persist across campaigns in the selected order Mara → Ivo → Ada → Noor; other profile progression remains open. Exclusive recipe pools follow the chosen unlocked mercenary without additional recipe unlock requirements.

**Confirmed continuation rules:** reversible Load, multiple shots and exclusive End Turn supersede the earlier single-shot turn. Non-Ammo activation and shot-versus-round recipe effects still need definition; the draft gun damage needs review under this flow. Crafting refunds, resolved-effect undo and exact loaded-state controls are not selected by allowing unloading.

## Build and persistence

An otherwise unspecified Utility damage bonus applies to the next shot only. Bonuses lasting the rest of the turn or fight must say so explicitly. Unused next-shot Utility damage bonuses expire at End Turn and do not carry over.

Recipe memory starts with a hard capacity of **20 recipes**, expandable by permanent upgrades and subject to balancing after beta testing; all stored recipes are visible without a deck/draw system. Start with **12 recipes: 8 shared and 4 character-specific**, leaving 8 free slots. Both the starter set and capacity are provisional values to balance through beta runs; individual recipe tuning remains draft. **Every new fight starts with all recipe cooldown counters cleared.** Numeric cooldowns start at recipe Use, whether producing a part or activating a Utility effect. A cooldown of N blocks the next N rounds naturally; automatic enemy-turn-end ticks skip the use round. Cooldown 1 used in round 4 is blocked round 5 and ready round 6; cooldown 2 is ready round 7. Explicit cooling can make either ready sooner; global cooling removes X from every cooling recipe. At zero counters, an affordable recipe can be used again immediately. No-cooldown recipes are once per turn unless a permanent upgrade says otherwise. Unused materials and parts carry between rounds but both clear at fight end. Recipes/upgrades persist for the current game. **Remaining player HP carries into the next fight; a fight transition does not automatically heal.** Explicit healing can change HP; healing availability and tuning remain to define. Shield resets after the complete enemy turn by default, unless a permanent upgrade explicitly says otherwise. Permanent upgrades may grant beginning Shield each turn, at fight start or on a specified turn; fresh grants do not automatically preserve old Shield. Recipe drafts do not supply reset exceptions. Upgrades may also grant fresh supplies on their defined triggers.

Accept desired loot items individually and Skip the remainder. Recipe rewards allow zero or one of three; at full memory, exchange a held recipe or skip without changing it. Cores sell for credits. Permanent claw upgrades come from shops or Officer loot and activate on purchase/acceptance. The 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Neither implicitly grants another Precision attempt.

Shops offer no direct healing service. They may sell healing recipes or permanent upgrades that increase maximum HP and heal for that same amount. For example, gaining +10 maximum HP changes 40/80 HP to 50/90. Buying a recipe does not itself activate its healing effect. Stock, prices and upgrade amounts remain to balance.

## Content and presentation

The catalogue has 606 drafts: 126 shared and 120 exclusive to each of four proposed mercenaries, for 246 possible recipes per character. Character identities, starter sizes, recipe numbers and detailed status rules remain proposals where the owner has not selected them.

Use the owner-supplied [style anchor](design/art%20anchor.png). Prepare in 16B's 2.5D side view with claw behind the gun and enemy intent ahead; use 16C's 3D perspective for action. Each bullet and shield interface must support at least 20 applied parts. The hard cap and physical-parts versus combined-effects presentation remain undecided. Illustrations and the old camera motion study are references only.

## Next proof and limits

Reconcile Utility catalogue dependencies, then resolve remaining build-effect activation, shot-versus-round effects and Load controls, supply amounts, enemy moves, shop stock/prices, fleeing rewards, other profile persistence and save/Game Over controls. Reconcile conflicting Shield recipes with the settled upgrade-only exceptions; do not reopen the base rule. Global cooling creates balance overlaps that need review. A proposed first demo is one mercenary, one city environment, about 30 recipes and a three-fight sample; this has not been selected and does not replace the full city wave.

The first playable should test whether different enemy intentions and supplies cause meaningful changes in crafting, saving, attack and defence, and whether an earned reward changes a later choice. Game feel and commercial appeal are untested. Naming research will screen current market relevance and public conflicts without treating an absence of search hits as legal clearance.
