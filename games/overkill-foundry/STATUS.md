# Overkill Foundry — independent game

## Current milestone — design Q&A toward a specified prototype demo

**Latest owner decision, 15 September:** starting recipe-memory capacity is **20**, expandable through permanent upgrades and explicitly subject to balancing after beta testing. This is capacity, not the number of recipes granted at the start. The catalogue's 12-recipe starter kit remains a proposal and would leave 8 free slots if selected. No beta build or balance evidence exists.

**Latest owner clarification, 14 September 2026:** Utility recipes activate immediately when used and produce **no part**; gun/Shield recipes produce parts. The previous question/audit wrongly assumed a finished Utility item. Cooldown 1 blocks the following round unless explicit cooling clears it; automatic ticks now skip the recipe-use round. These corrections supersede the stored-Utility and fresh-counter same-round-tick conventions. Shield reset and permanent-upgrade exceptions remain settled.

**Owner decisions, 14 September 2026:** the mercenary/robot recipe-combat design is a whole new game and will use a fresh gameplay implementation. **Latest turn-flow Q&A:** Klaus confirms unloading and changing selected parts after Load, before Fire. He then selects **End Turn** as the exclusive action ending the player turn: multiple shots are allowed while parts/resources and recipe availability permit; Fire returns to preparation while combat continues. This replaces the earlier Fire-immediately-into-enemy-turn flow. Shots do not restart collection, reset recipe uses, tick cooldowns or clear Shield. Fired parts are consumed. Utility recipes now activate directly on Use without a part; define remaining build-effect activation and shot-versus-round effect behaviour next; no added firing cost, energy budget or shot cap is selected.

## Settled design and remaining questions

Use [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) for the concise loop and [the design plan](design/REDESIGN-PLAN.md) for owner decisions. Preserve the mercenary/city campaign, 10–20-fight waves, recipe memory, no deck/draw, rear claw, one collection per round and one optional Precision grab per fight. Load precedes target selection and Fire, and can be undone before firing. Multiple shots are allowed within a turn; Fire returns to preparation and only End Turn hands play to enemies, including after a zero-shot turn. Enemies follow visible intent. Round Shield/builds reset after enemy actions. Automatic cooldown ticks skip the recipe-use round: cooldown 1 used in round 4 blocks round 5 and is ready round 6; cooldown 2 blocks rounds 5–6 and is ready round 7. Explicit cooling affects every cooling recipe and can make it ready sooner. Unused parts/resources persist across rounds and both clear after a fight.

Permanent claw upgrades are bought or accepted from Officer loot and activate immediately. All 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Recipe loot allows zero or one from three; skip freely, or exchange at full memory. Other loot acceptance is separate. New-game/profile persistence, fleeing rewards and exact economic values remain open.

**Catalogue:** 606 recipes, 126 shared plus 120 exclusive per proposed mercenary; each can discover 246, subject to memory capacity. Costs, stats and character conventions remain drafts. The previous pass revised 34 cooling-effect rows globally without changing costs/cooldown numbers; SH068/SH100 and SH099/SH116 now have identical effects and need balance/identity review. Thirteen recipe-granted Shield-retention drafts are superseded; related expiry/conversion and delayed Shield drafts need reconciliation with the settled upgrade rule before inclusion in a demo. This is content work, not an unresolved base-rule choice. No other old implementation rules become this game's rules.

**Visuals:** [art anchor.png](design/art%20anchor.png) is STYLE, not gameplay screenshots. 16B anchors planning with the claw behind the gun and enemy intent ahead; 16C anchors attack perspective. Direct Utility Use, corrected cooldowns, reversible Load, multiple shots, exclusive End Turn and Shield reset/upgrade exceptions remain settled. No game implementation or playtest exists.
