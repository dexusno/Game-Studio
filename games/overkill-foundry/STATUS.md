# Overkill Foundry — independent game

## Current milestone — separate game and selected name

**Owner decisions, 14 September 2026:** the mercenary/robot recipe-combat design is a whole new game and will use a fresh gameplay implementation. After requesting market-informed naming and actual conflict checks, Klaus selected **Overkill Foundry**. The catalogue ID and directory are `overkill-foundry`; the temporary `scrap-combat` record was renamed before integration. No exact game title surfaced in the checked stores; completed US/Norway/TMview phrase searches found no exact candidate mark. FOUNDRY and OVERKILL Software are existing nearby names; WIPO access failed. No global non-use or legal-clearance claim, registration or publication was made.

**Separation:** design plan, 606 recipes, redesign decision history, strategy/balance research, style anchor, concept boards and the standalone Blender camera study have been carried from Magnet Sweep at revision `adf3a3c55e2c680f5f6a2b442d0ed93cd84c4883`. Magnet Sweep is a parked historical project. Windows/Unreal is the planned direction for a new project; current stage is **concept**.

## Settled design and remaining questions

Use [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) for the concise loop and [the design plan](design/REDESIGN-PLAN.md) for owner decisions. Preserve the mercenary/city campaign, 10–20-fight waves, recipe memory, no deck/draw, rear claw, one collection per round and one optional Precision grab per fight. Load precedes target selection and Fire; End Turn / Defend permits a no-shot round. Enemies follow visible intent. Round Shield/builds reset after enemy actions, fresh counters tick then, and cooling affects every cooling recipe. Cooldown 2 crafted round 4 is ready round 6. Unused parts/resources persist across rounds and both clear after a fight.

Permanent claw upgrades are bought or accepted from Officer loot and activate immediately. All 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Recipe loot allows zero or one from three; skip freely, or exchange at full memory. Other loot acceptance is separate. New-game/profile persistence, fleeing rewards and exact economic values remain open.

**Catalogue:** 606 recipes, 126 shared plus 120 exclusive per proposed mercenary; each can discover 246, subject to memory capacity. Costs, stats and character conventions remain drafts. The latest pass revised 34 cooling-effect rows globally without changing costs/cooldown numbers; SH068/SH100 and SH099/SH116 now have identical effects and need balance/identity review. Existing Shield-retention rows remain unapproved exception candidates. No other old implementation rules become this game's rules.

**Visuals:** [art anchor.png](design/art%20anchor.png) is STYLE, not gameplay screenshots. 16B anchors planning with the claw behind the gun and enemy intent ahead; 16C anchors attack perspective. Each city has unique surroundings. Support at least 20 parts per bullet and per shield; the final cap and effects-summary/physical-part treatment remain open. The preserved camera study establishes only its historical art-test observations, not this game's runtime quality.

**Next:** resume owner design Q&A on Shield-retention exceptions, Load/undo and secondary-effect activation before numeric supply/enemy/economy work. Naming and directory separation are complete; do not restart the naming search unless the owner requests it or new conflict evidence appears. One-mercenary/three-fight demo scope is a proposal, not an implementation authorization. No build or playtest of this game exists.
