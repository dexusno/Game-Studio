**Latest turn-flow Q&A:** Klaus confirms unloading and changing selected parts after Load, before Fire. ## Settled design and remaining questions

Use [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) for the concise loop and [the design plan](design/REDESIGN-PLAN.md) for owner decisions. Preserve the mercenary/city campaign, 10–20-fight waves, recipe memory, no deck/draw, rear claw, one collection per round and one optional Precision grab per fight. Load precedes target selection and Fire, and can be undone before firing. Multiple shots are allowed within a turn; Fire returns to preparation and only End Turn hands play to enemies, including after a zero-shot turn. Enemies follow visible intent. Round Shield/builds reset after enemy actions, fresh counters tick then, and cooling affects every cooling recipe. Cooldown 2 crafted round 4 is ready round 6. Unused parts/resources persist across rounds and both clear after a fight.

Permanent claw upgrades are bought or accepted from Officer loot and activate immediately. All 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Recipe loot allows zero or one from three; skip freely, or exchange at full memory. Other loot acceptance is separate. New-game/profile persistence, fleeing rewards and exact economic values remain open.
