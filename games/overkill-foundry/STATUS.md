## Settled design and remaining questions

Use [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) for the concise loop and [the design plan](design/REDESIGN-PLAN.md) for owner decisions. Preserve the mercenary/city campaign, 10–20-fight waves, recipe memory, no deck/draw, rear claw, one collection per round and one optional Precision grab per fight. Load precedes target selection and Fire; End Turn / Defend permits a no-shot round. Enemies follow visible intent. Round Shield/builds reset after enemy actions, fresh counters tick then, and cooling affects every cooling recipe. Cooldown 2 crafted round 4 is ready round 6. Unused parts/resources persist across rounds and both clear after a fight.

Permanent claw upgrades are bought or accepted from Officer loot and activate immediately. All 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Recipe loot allows zero or one from three; skip freely, or exchange at full memory. Other loot acceptance is separate. New-game/profile persistence, fleeing rewards and exact economic values remain open.

**Catalogue:** 606 recipes, 126 shared plus 120 exclusive per proposed mercenary; each can discover 246, subject to memory capacity. Costs, stats and character conventions remain drafts. The previous pass revised 34 cooling-effect rows globally without changing costs/cooldown numbers; SH068/SH100 and SH099/SH116 now have identical effects and need balance/identity review. Thirteen recipe-granted Shield-retention drafts are superseded; related expiry/conversion and delayed Shield drafts need reconciliation with the settled upgrade rule before inclusion in a demo. This is content work, not an unresolved base-rule choice. No other old implementation rules become this game's rules.
