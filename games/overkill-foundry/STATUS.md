# Overkill Foundry — independent game

## Current milestone — design Q&A toward a specified prototype demo

**Latest owner decision, 15 September:** the character unlock order is **Mara → Ivo → Ada → Noor**. Mara is initially selectable; clearing a city with Mara unlocks Ivo, with Ivo unlocks Ada, and with Ada unlocks Noor. A city victory is sufficient; a full campaign victory is not required. Earned character access persists in the active profile across campaigns. The order is selected; detailed character identities, traits and balance remain draft. Recipes have no additional permanent unlock requirements within the chosen mercenary's eligible pool. The selected starting set remains 12 recipes. Seen offers enter Collection even if skipped, and that history survives death. Death still ends the current campaign and requires a new game, with the selected gloating evil-AI Game Over presentation; the defeated campaign cannot be retried/resumed through the ordinary flow.

**Starting memory and recipes, selected 15 September:** start with **12 recipes: 8 shared and 4 character-specific**, within **20 memory slots**, leaving **8 free slots**. Use the catalogue's listed starter sets for now. Klaus explicitly wants both the starter set and capacity balanced through beta runs; these are selected initial values, not tested final tuning. Permanent upgrades may expand memory. No beta build or balance evidence exists.

**Latest owner clarification, 14 September 2026:** Utility recipes activate immediately when used and produce **no part**; gun/Shield recipes produce parts. The previous question/audit wrongly assumed a finished Utility item. Cooldown 1 blocks the following round unless explicit cooling clears it; automatic ticks now skip the recipe-use round. These corrections supersede the stored-Utility and fresh-counter same-round-tick conventions. Shield reset and permanent-upgrade exceptions remain settled.

**Owner decisions, 14 September 2026:** the mercenary/robot recipe-combat design is a whole new game and will use a fresh gameplay implementation. After requesting market-informed naming and actual conflict checks, Klaus selected **Overkill Foundry**. The catalogue ID and directory are `overkill-foundry`; the temporary `scrap-combat` record was renamed before integration. [Naming study](design/NAMING-STUDY.md) records the recommendation, competing judgments, completed searches and limits. No exact game title surfaced in the checked stores; completed US/Norway/TMview phrase searches found no exact candidate mark. FOUNDRY and OVERKILL Software are existing nearby names; WIPO access failed. No global non-use or legal-clearance claim, registration or publication was made.

**Separation:** design plan, 606 recipes, redesign decision history, strategy/balance research, style anchor, concept boards and the standalone Blender camera study have been carried from Magnet Sweep at revision `adf3a3c55e2c680f5f6a2b442d0ed93cd84c4883`. Art binaries retain their original bytes and provenance. No old gameplay source, Unreal project, saves, audio/game assets, binaries or engine-build evidence were copied. Magnet Sweep is a parked historical project. Windows/Unreal is the planned direction for a new project; current stage is **concept**.

**Latest turn-flow Q&A:** Klaus confirms unloading and changing selected parts after Load, before Fire. He then selects **End Turn** as the exclusive action ending the player turn: multiple shots are allowed while parts/resources and recipe availability permit; Fire returns to preparation while combat continues. This replaces the earlier Fire-immediately-into-enemy-turn flow. Shots do not restart collection, reset recipe uses, tick cooldowns or clear Shield. Fired parts are consumed. Utility recipes now activate directly on Use without a part; define remaining build-effect activation and shot-versus-round effect behaviour next; no added firing cost, energy budget or shot cap is selected.

## Settled design and remaining questions

Use [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) for the concise loop and [the design plan](design/REDESIGN-PLAN.md) for owner decisions. Preserve the mercenary/city campaign, 10–20-fight waves, recipe memory, no deck/draw, rear claw, one collection per round and one optional Precision grab per fight. Load precedes target selection and Fire, and can be undone before firing. Multiple shots are allowed within a turn; Fire returns to preparation and only End Turn hands play to enemies, including after a zero-shot turn. Enemies follow visible intent. Round Shield/builds reset after enemy actions. Automatic cooldown ticks skip the recipe-use round: cooldown 1 used in round 4 blocks round 5 and is ready round 6; cooldown 2 blocks rounds 5–6 and is ready round 7. Explicit cooling affects every cooling recipe and can make it ready sooner. Unused parts/resources persist across rounds and both clear after a fight.

Permanent claw upgrades are bought or accepted from Officer loot and activate immediately. All 23 temporary next-round gathering recipes remain alongside them. Recipe loot allows zero or one from three; skip freely, or exchange at full memory. Other loot acceptance is separate. Recipe offers count as Collection discoveries even when skipped, and that history survives death. Recipe offers need no permanent unlocks. Character access follows city-clear unlocks in the selected order Mara → Ivo → Ada → Noor. Other profile persistence, fleeing rewards and exact economic values remain open.

**Catalogue:** 606 recipes, 126 shared plus 120 exclusive per proposed mercenary; each can discover 246, while only the active crafting set is limited by memory capacity. Costs, stats and character conventions remain drafts. The previous pass revised 34 cooling-effect rows globally without changing costs/cooldown numbers; SH068/SH100 and SH099/SH116 now have identical effects and need balance/identity review. Thirteen recipe-granted Shield-retention drafts are superseded; related expiry/conversion and delayed Shield drafts need reconciliation with the settled upgrade rule before inclusion in a demo.
