# Overkill Foundry — independent game

## Current milestone — design Q&A toward a specified prototype demo

**New Game economy reset, selected 15 September:** a campaign is one New Game spanning the selected three-city route. Starting a New Game resets credits to the starting balance; credits from the previous campaign do not carry over. Credits remain available across fights and cities within the same campaign. The starting amount is still to balance, not implicitly zero. This leaves profile Collection history and character unlocks intact. Continue remains continuation of the existing campaign, including the selected fight-entry replay/seed rule. Starting a New Game also clears all unsold energy cores from the previous campaign. Cores remain available between fights and cities in the current campaign until sold.

**Part resale correction, 15 September:** each part has a base sale value derived from the normal resource cost of producing **one of that part through its main recipe**. Keep the selected current shop resource prices, 50% sale factor and whole-credit round-down: `floor(0.5 × sum(main-recipe ingredient quantity for one part × current shop resource unit price))`. Rare-recipe resource rebates or discounts do not reduce its sale value. Producing the same part through another recipe, in a batch or by copying also does not change its reference cost. Different outputs each use their own main-recipe value. **This supersedes the earlier actual-batch equal-cost allocation rule and resolves the pending mixed-output pricing question.** For example, a normal one-part ingredient value of 10 credits gives a 5-credit sale value even if an alternative recipe spends only 6 credits producing that part. Parts without a defined main recipe/one-part baseline need a content reference before pricing; do not invent it from the discounted producing recipe. Raw materials cannot be sold; energy-core and unused-crafted-part sales remain allowed. Selling removes the part while its recipe remains in memory. Utilities create no saleable Utility part, and unused parts/resources still clear at fight end without automatic sale.

**Shop stock within fights, selected 15 September:** the shop keeps the same stock throughout a fight. Closing/reopening the shop, firing or advancing to another round does not refresh its offers or reset purchases already made. Recipe/Shop access while loaded remains available. The shop restocks after each completed fight, with the refreshed stock available for subsequent shopping while the campaign continues. Restocking occurs once for that fight completion; merely reopening the shop does not repeat it. Shop offers have limited quantities: purchases reduce their remaining stock, and an offer that sells out cannot be bought again until restocking. Exact stock counts, prices and the detailed offer-refresh method remain open; no universal one-unit count is selected. Noncombat Mystery visits are not selected as an additional restock trigger by this answer.

**Duplicate recipe memory, selected 15 September:** memory may hold two copies of the exact same recipe, with each copy occupying one slot. For example, two Flat Plate entries consume two of the initial 20 slots. Capacity still applies. Each stored copy independently tracks its cooldown and once-per-turn use. Using one leaves another ready copy available, with every use paying its normal costs. Two Flat Plate copies may each be used once that turn. The existing cooldown duration, use-round tick exemption and global cooling apply to each copy; this does not multiply explicitly non-stacking effects or grant additional uses to an already-used no-cooldown copy. The 12-recipe starter sets remain unchanged.

**Recipe-defined targeting, selected 15 September:** each recipe decides which targets receive its status effects, including for multi-target bullets. There is no blanket main-target-only or all-hit-targets rule. Recipe text must make its scope clear; missing or ambiguous scope needs a content correction, not a hidden targeting default. Existing explicit target restrictions remain attached to their recipes.

**Latest owner rule, 15 September:** the gun has **zero base damage**. All bullet damage and effects come from the parts assembled into it; the gun supplies no innate damage or shot effects. This supersedes the catalogue's +4 draft. Parts adding 6 and 3 supply 9 total together or split, before other effects. Draft Hot Barrel/Charged Barrel damage grants are flagged for reconciliation with this source rule; existing Utility activation and selected bonus durations are not silently rewritten.

**Recipe-cost analysis completed, 15 September:** Klaus confirms gathering already has a steering choice and requests a costed beta haul inspired by Slay the Spire balancing. The [current analysis](design/BALANCE-RESEARCH.md#current-beta-haul-recommendation--15-september-2026) recommends **10 materials per normal haul: 8 baseline + 2 steered**, with a proposed baseline of 3 Iron, 2 Copper, 1 Carbon, 1 Glass and 1 Circuit. This is a beta recommendation, not an owner-selected value. All 606 printed recipe costs were analysed, and all four 12-recipe starter kits were compared across 6/8/10/12-material fixtures. Average costs are 1.67 at Base, 2.36 Common, 3.17 Uncommon, 4.12 Rare and 5.24 Legendary. The script checks cost affordability, not combat legality or outcomes; extra effect costs, refunds and cooldown reuse are outside that probe. Extra Lift's 2-for-2 delayed return and material bottlenecks need separate review. The owner has since rejected base gun damage; the cost-only results are unaffected. Recipe rows are unchanged; no gameplay implementation or beta evidence is claimed.

**Latest owner decision, 15 September:** individual bullets and shields have **no fixed part-count cap; builds are only resource restricted**. Available materials and crafted parts, under existing recipe use/cooldown requirements, constrain assembly. Unused-material and crafted-part reserves also have **no storage cap within a fight**; both pools carry between rounds and clear at fight end. The separate starting recipe-memory capacity remains 20. Crafting and firing use **no separate global energy/action-point budget**. Materials, available parts and recipe use/cooldown requirements provide the action limits. This supersedes the earlier additional-energy proposal and confirms the catalogue's no-extra-energy assumption; numerical costs and supply values remain draft. The interface must accommodate larger valid builds; the earlier minimum of 20 parts per bullet and shield is not a cap.

**Loaded-state access, selected 15 September:** Recipe and Shop remain available after Load, before Fire. The player can craft available recipes, use Utilities and make permitted purchases without first unloading; costs, cooldowns and other normal requirements still apply. Closing either view returns to the loaded preparation state. Parts in a loaded bullet cannot be sold until that bullet is unloaded. Other eligible unused parts remain saleable while the bullet is loaded. End Turn unloads any unfired bullet and returns its unused parts to the available part pool; no assembled, loaded bullet carries into the next player turn. This does not fire or consume those parts, refund crafting materials, or preserve next-shot bonuses that expire at End Turn. Other loaded-bullet reservation/validation and secondary-effect ordering still need definition.

**Shield activation correction, 15 September:** **only End Turn activates the prepared shield**, before enemy actions, regardless of whether the player fired zero, one or several shots. Crafting, placing parts, Load and Fire leave that shield pending. Fire consumes bullet parts and returns to preparation. Prepared Shield values add when End Turn activates them: preparing 8 and then 5 during the turn gives 13 at End Turn, assuming no other changes; neither contribution activates on a shot. Protection lasts through the enemy turn and follows the existing reset/upgrade exceptions. Explicit upgrade Shield grants retain their stated timing. This supersedes the earlier Fire-activation rule, zero-shot-only exception and per-shot activation example. Exact used-part/UI accounting and secondary-effect ordering remain open.

**Enemy escape, selected 15 September:** a robot announces its planned escape **4 turns in advance**, shown with its enemy intent. Robots continue their normal attacks and abilities while the warning counts down, until the departure turn; show the escape countdown alongside their current action intent. On the departure turn, escaping is the robot's action and replaces its normal attack or ability; show Escape as that turn's intent. It does not take another normal action before fleeing. Exact countdown display/tick conventions remain to define. Fleeing enemy robots leave no energy cores or recipe loot. If every enemy escapes without any kills, there are no cores or recipe rewards from that fight. Killed enemies remain eligible for their existing loot even if other robots flee. The existing fight-end condition still applies when no enemies remain in the fight; exact loot amounts and offer tuning remain open.

**Mystery progress, selected 15 September:** a completed non-combat Mystery visit counts as one step toward the city boss. The city wave is therefore **10–20 encounters**, including non-combat Mystery visits and the final boss, rather than 10–20 mandatory fights. A chosen Mystery counts once whether its branch is combat, a merchant or tech support; ordinary shop revisits do not advance city progress. Each city ends with a single boss encounter option, replacing the normal three encounter choices. The boss stage does not offer Regular/Officer/Mystery alternatives; the guaranteed Regular option applies only to ordinary encounter choices. Boss identities and supporting enemies remain to design. A full campaign contains **3 cities**, as the initial value to balance in beta. Each city retains its 10–20-step wave ending in a boss, now counting both combat and non-combat encounters. Cities 1 and 2 lead onward; clearing city 3 completes the three-city route. Exact finale presentation/rewards, per-city tuning and detailed Mystery outcomes remain open. This does not select the proposed demo sample or authorize implementation.

**New-game replacement, selected 15 September:** Start New Game asks for confirmation before replacing an unfinished campaign in the active profile. Confirming allows the new campaign to replace the old one; cancelling keeps the current campaign intact. Profile Collection history and character unlocks persist. Each profile holds at most one ongoing campaign. Continue targets that campaign; separate profiles retain their own independent campaigns, Collection records and character unlocks. Starting another campaign in the same profile cannot create a second ongoing slot. Replacement requires confirmation; exact dialog wording and placement remain to design. No save data is changed by this design decision.

**Continue behaviour, selected 15 September:** quitting during an unfinished fight makes Continue restart that fight from the beginning with the same seed. Restore the original fight-entry campaign state, including entry HP, recipes/upgrades and supplies; the player plays the fight again from that checkpoint. Preserve the encounter and seed: repeating the same choices reproduces random results, while different choices can change the outcome. This applies to an unfinished campaign; death still ends the campaign. Exact save implementation and between-fight resume details remain open.

**Character unlock order, selected 15 September:** the character unlock order is **Mara → Ivo → Ada → Noor**. Mara is initially selectable; clearing a city with Mara unlocks Ivo, with Ivo unlocks Ada, and with Ada unlocks Noor. A city victory is sufficient; a full campaign victory is not required. Earned character access persists in the active profile across campaigns. The order is selected; detailed character identities, traits and balance remain draft. Recipes have no additional permanent unlock requirements within the chosen mercenary's eligible pool. The selected starting set remains 12 recipes. Seen offers enter Collection even if skipped, and that history survives death. Death still ends the current campaign and requires a new game, with the selected gloating evil-AI Game Over presentation; the defeated campaign cannot be retried/resumed through the ordinary flow.

**Starting memory and recipes, selected 15 September:** start with **12 recipes: 8 shared and 4 character-specific**, within **20 memory slots**, leaving **8 free slots**. Use the catalogue's listed starter sets for now. Klaus explicitly wants both the starter set and capacity balanced through beta runs; these are selected initial values, not tested final tuning. Permanent upgrades may expand memory. No beta build or balance evidence exists.

**Latest owner clarification, 14 September 2026:** Utility recipes activate immediately when used and produce **no part**; gun/Shield recipes produce parts. The previous question/audit wrongly assumed a finished Utility item. Cooldown 1 blocks the following round unless explicit cooling clears it; automatic ticks now skip the recipe-use round. These corrections supersede the stored-Utility and fresh-counter same-round-tick conventions. Shield reset and permanent-upgrade exceptions remain settled.

**Owner decisions, 14 September 2026:** the mercenary/robot recipe-combat design is a whole new game and will use a fresh gameplay implementation. After requesting market-informed naming and actual conflict checks, Klaus selected **Overkill Foundry**. The catalogue ID and directory are `overkill-foundry`; the temporary `scrap-combat` record was renamed before integration. [Naming study](design/NAMING-STUDY.md) records the recommendation, competing judgments, completed searches and limits. No exact game title surfaced in the checked stores; completed US/Norway/TMview phrase searches found no exact candidate mark. FOUNDRY and OVERKILL Software are existing nearby names; WIPO access failed. No global non-use or legal-clearance claim, registration or publication was made.

**Latest turn-flow Q&A:** Klaus confirms unloading and changing selected parts after Load, before Fire. He then selects **End Turn** as the exclusive action ending the player turn: multiple shots are allowed while parts/resources and recipe availability permit; Fire returns to preparation while combat continues. This replaces the earlier Fire-immediately-into-enemy-turn flow. Shots do not restart collection, reset recipe uses, tick cooldowns or clear Shield. Fired parts are consumed. Utility recipes now activate directly on Use without a part; define remaining build-effect activation and shot-versus-round effect behaviour next; a separate global energy/action-point budget is rejected; no additional firing material charge or hard shot cap is selected.
