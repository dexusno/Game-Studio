Enemy work owns DBEnemy.*, DBProjectile.* and DBCombatEffect.*. Art owns source geometry/import scripts. QA owns DBShieldChecks.*. Root launches Unreal and integrates.

Every piece has one index 0–5 and an EDBShieldPieceState: Attached, Selected, Outbound, Lodged, Returning or Regenerating. Public queries expose counts, state, progress and its actual ADBThrownShield actor. Attached count includes selected pieces, but selection disables guarding. IsShieldAway means any deployment, never a global held-action gate. Legacy ShieldState/ThrownShield summarize activity for older callers; do not use them as the authoritative piece ledger.

PressFire snapshots eligible attached identities. After 0.22s, pieces light at 0.316s intervals; full six-piece commitment takes 1.8s. Release launches actual lit identities. New catches/rebuilds attach unlit and become eligible on the next hold. RMB cancels selection; release cannot leak a canceled attack. Q always recalls; F uses Special for heavy/Ram. Each frontal ordinary block spends one piece, with attack identity deduplication. A 3s independent rebuild starts immediately. Capacitor shortens new timers to 2.6s. Ram II advances one rebuild by 0.85s once per landed heavy/rush. These are provisional balance values, not owner-approved final tuning.

Rest/weapon mode folds the six full-size meshes along the forearm in two staggered banks. RMB unfolds into a shield through staggered hinges with a small settling motion; releasing guard refolds. Catch transforms follow the current physical socket/orientation. Full charge requires all six eligible pieces and the complete hold, and grants a bonus only when all six projectiles spawn. Direct hits gain 1.65x damage and flight speed; the volley can emit one 80-damage, 340cm-radius burst against at most eight visible targets. A shared volley identity bounds and deduplicates that burst. Return hits do not repeat it.

Tap melee alternates sweeps with a stronger third hit, approximately 2.8–3m reach and a capsule-swept 50cm step. Direction and visibility checks prevent striking through cover. F retains heavy/Ram, and the core remains useful at zero pieces. Charge has its own looping audio component with rising pitch/level, ready cue and cancellation/menu cleanup. Contact and catch voices have short global gates and voice limits; full release, melee and dash use distinct assets. Root owns these integrated files after worker handoff.

ADBThrownShield owns a single segment's flight, outward/return victim sets, cover sweeps, bearer path and non-damaging emergency recovery. Catch/destruction resolves the matching piece once. Simultaneous flight and held defense are supported. Menu pause freezes flight/rebuild; reset removes all deployed actors and restores six pieces. The central hub/core/grip always remains and can strike with no pieces.

Mirror stores force for physical contacts. Frost outbound hits chill; held/return impacts shatter. Ember/Storm, Echo/Split/Stormfracture and Capacitor retain finite secondary contacts. ## Scene and progression
DBRecoveryScene assembles three octagonal sanctuaries on a 4200cm grid. A seeded direction/turn and three court variants preserve a continuous route. Central paving, soil and clustered planting, a raised spring/rill, connected bridge gateways, enclosing masonry and layered exterior landforms give the generator structure. Exactly four traversable arches serve the two bridge links; no standalone dead-end arches are spawned. Hidden floors remain continuous under uneven decorative surfaces, and the closed gates span the full 700cm bridge deck. Protected encounter/practice pads and capsule lanes constrain decoration. Later courts have a usable eight-tread side terrace. This is a bounded modular procedural system, not full multi-biome generation. Enemies use capsule sweeps/local steering and do not need a baked navmesh.

E starts each court's encounter. First clear offers Anchor/Mirror/Ram, second offers Frost/Ember/Storm; last has a heavy sentinel and caster. Claiming a reward opens the passage and stays in the current court. Entering the next court clears practice, sets its checkpoint and enables its ward. Entry gates close during active later encounters. Final clear removes hostile bolts and saves terminal completion immediately; the short victory delay is presentation only.

DBRewardPractice creates RoomId=-1 actors after actual acquisition. bPracticeTarget enemies retain status/reaction but stay still and reset on lethal damage; their kills never advance encounters. Mirror/Anchor adds a live caster. Health is safe during practice. Cleanup removes owned bolts, actors and transient effects on reset/transition/encounter start. The saved RoomRewards map restores the correct practice on resume.

Default profile DreamboundSegments is separate from DreamboundShieldStudy and DreamboundBeta. CRC journal slots, earned patterns, selected elements and settings persist. Master sound level is an additive save property defaulting to 85%; pause provides plus/minus and mute. Existing input bindings and sensitivity remain intact. Mid-fight exit preserves the encounter-entry checkpoint; cleared or unstarted encounters can save current progress. QA requires an isolated slot and restores its previous bytes. DBShieldChecks uses ordinary world ticks, never recursive world ticking.

## Grounded movement and biological enemies
Walk speed is 590cm/s; Shift sprint is 885cm/s. Stamina drains at 20/s only during measured ground displacement and recovers at 25/s after a 0.8s delay. Exhaustion requires a fresh Shift press with at least 20 stamina. Left Alt starts a 1850cm/s horizontal CharacterMovement root-motion burst for 0.24s with a 1.45s cooldown, release latch and independent 0.13s evasion window. Collision sweeps stop it at walls; leaving the floor cancels the burst and keeps gravity. Pause/focus/reset clears intent and active burst. Low-amplitude velocity-driven camera translation and weapon gait preserve mouse aim; landing compresses the view briefly.

DBEnemy loads continuous 12-bone skinned biological meshes under /Game/Art/OrganicEnemies. Briarhide serves melee/hunter/heavy roles with scale and motion variants; MireSeer serves casters. Procedural phase motion drives spine/head, arms/elbows and thighs/knees with breathing/recoil/collapse. Attack and capsule logic retain their existing contracts. There is no authored animation library, foot IK, ragdoll or facial rig. Names/lore remain proposals.

DBRecoveryScene adds twelve original LivingWorld mesh families in wet spring and dry ruin clusters, with foliage/stone/ceramic surface detail. Root probes and protected gameplay footprints constrain planting; water lilies use the actual basin surface. Instanced non-colliding details cull at 65m. Dragonflies/moths use small material motion; no animal simulation or new complete biome is implied. See art/living-world and art/organic-enemies for reproducible source and import records.
