## Runtime ownership
Integration owns DBGameMode.*, DBRecoveryScene.cpp, DBRewardPractice.cpp, DBMotionDemo.cpp, DBHUD.*, configuration, packaging and shared records. Combat owns DBCharacter.* and DBThrownShield.*. Enemy work owns DBEnemy.*, DBProjectile.* and DBCombatEffect.*. Art owns source geometry/import scripts. QA owns DBShieldChecks.*. Root launches Unreal and integrates.

Every piece has one index 0–5 and an EDBShieldPieceState: Attached, Selected, Outbound, Lodged, Returning or Regenerating. Public queries expose counts, state, progress and its actual ADBThrownShield actor. Attached count includes selected pieces, but selection disables guarding. IsShieldAway means any deployment, never a global held-action gate. Legacy ShieldState/ThrownShield summarize activity for older callers; do not use them as the authoritative piece ledger.

PressFire snapshots eligible attached identities. After 0.22s, pieces light at 0.14s intervals. Release launches actual lit identities. New catches/rebuilds attach unlit and become eligible on the next hold. RMB cancels selection; release cannot leak a canceled attack. Q always recalls; F uses Special for heavy/Ram. Each frontal ordinary block spends one piece, with attack identity deduplication. A 3s independent rebuild starts immediately. Capacitor shortens new timers to 2.6s. Ram II advances one rebuild by 0.85s once per landed heavy/rush. These are provisional balance values, not owner-approved final tuning.

ADBThrownShield owns a single segment's flight, outward/return victim sets, cover sweeps, bearer path and non-damaging emergency recovery. Catch/destruction resolves the matching piece once. Simultaneous flight and held defense are supported. Menu pause freezes flight/rebuild; reset removes all deployed actors and restores six pieces. The central hub/core/grip always remains and can strike with no pieces.

Mirror stores force for physical contacts. Frost outbound hits chill; held/return impacts shatter. Ember/Storm, Echo/Split/Stormfracture and Capacitor retain finite secondary contacts. Character descriptions specify current rank behavior. Enemy element geometry persists independently of generic hit tint. Finite DBCombatEffect actors retain Storm arcs after source death; they are capped and expire with room/run changes. The boss offers a warned one-piece frontal interception via TryInterceptShieldPiece; flight owns destruction. Projectile Anchor tests sort actual intersections across all deployed pieces and consume each bolt once.

## Scene and progression
DBRecoveryScene assembles three physically distinct square courts on a 4200cm grid. A seeded initial direction and turn vary their route; each court selects one of three cover arrangements. Protected door axes, continuous underfloors and corner decoration support all variants. This is a bounded modular procedural system, not full multi-biome generation. Enemies use capsule sweeps/local steering and do not need a baked navmesh.

E starts each court's encounter. First clear offers Anchor/Mirror/Ram, second offers Frost/Ember/Storm; last has a heavy sentinel and caster. Claiming a reward opens the passage and stays in the current court. Entering the next court clears practice, sets its checkpoint and enables its ward. Entry gates close during active later encounters. Final clear removes hostile bolts and saves terminal completion immediately; the short victory delay is presentation only.

DBRewardPractice creates RoomId=-1 actors after actual acquisition. bPracticeTarget enemies retain status/reaction but stay still and reset on lethal damage; their kills never advance encounters. Mirror/Anchor adds a live caster. Health is safe during practice. Cleanup removes owned bolts, actors and transient effects on reset/transition/encounter start. The saved RoomRewards map restores the correct practice on resume.

Default profile DreamboundSegments is separate from DreamboundShieldStudy and DreamboundBeta. CRC journal slots, earned patterns, selected elements and settings persist. Mid-fight exit preserves the encounter-entry checkpoint; cleared or unstarted encounters can save current progress. QA requires an isolated slot and restores its previous bytes. DBShieldChecks uses ordinary world ticks, never recursive world ticking.
