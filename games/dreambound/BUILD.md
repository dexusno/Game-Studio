## Enemy motion review

`scripts/Capture-EnemyMotion.ps1 -Build Editor -Creature Melee -View Side -CaptureDirectory <new-private-directory>` captures the actual non-practice enemy AI against a choreographed, protected player target. Creature choices are Melee, Caster, Hunter and Boss; views are Side, LowSide, Front, Player and Detail. LowSide exposes near-floor contact; Detail follows the visible facing for mouth/hand deformation review. Boss Front includes the complete raised claw arc. Use `-Build Shipping` for CreatureAnatomy2, or explicitly select another `-OutputName`. Each run has an isolated QA save/profile and preserves prior captures by requiring a new output directory.

`-Rig Default` is the capture default and passes no rig override; current Editor source selects Dread and the retained Shipping package selects Anatomy. `-Rig Dread` explicitly requests the facial refinement, `-Rig Anatomy` requests the retained38/43-bone sources, while `-Rig Performance` and `-Rig Legacy` select retained predecessors. The [anatomy contract](art/organic-enemies/animation/anatomy-contract.json) records source hashes, axes, channels and preserved geometry. `-Idle` exercises a real dormant creature's normal visual tick outside the active room. For Editor material diagnosis, `-Surface BaseColor`, `Roughness` or `Specular` selects the engine's VisualizeBuffer view; `Lit` is normal rendering. A rejected debug-view command invalidates that diagnostic. These isolated studies establish the stated observations only, not reference parity or ordinary play.

The default32-second study includes approach, stopping/turns, attacks, physical hits at20/22 seconds, lethal damage at28 seconds and settling. `-Seconds 8` limits a focused regression to eight seconds; the report lists actual phase coverage. Boss starts outside ranged distance to expose its gait and relocates the scripted player once onto nearby paving at14 seconds to provoke normal close Slam selection. That relocation is recorded; it is not native player input. `-FootMarkers` adds world ankle-target markers for Editor diagnosis only. The observer runs after enemy movement/posing and before the engine camera-manager update, avoiding a one-frame lag in the review camera.

Each PNG is one rendered simulation tick at fixed30Hz. `python scripts/review_enemy_motion.py <capture>/Saved/EnemyMotionStudy/Melee-Side --video <capture>/review.mp4` validates actual images/CSV cadence, measures contacts/body motion and encodes those consecutive frames without duplication. Clips are silent. These runs establish observed motion cases and exact build identity, not hardware frame rate, audio quality or an ordinary playthrough. Keep raw frames and Invocation.json in ignored local storage; retain compact review reports with the relevant build evidence.

The retained Performance source is prepared with Blender using `scripts/prepare_organic_enemy.py --asset Briarhide --source <original-textured.glb> --prepared-source <finished-motion/SK_OE_Briarhide.blend> --output <finished-performance> --preview` after Blender's `--` separator; MireSeer uses its matching asset/source. Build the current anatomy from that immutable Performance source with Blender `--background --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/refine_organic_anatomy.py -- --asset Briarhide`, then the matching `--asset MireSeer` run. The script resolves the private output root from config.local.json and writes finished-anatomy. The selective full-editor importer is `scripts/import_organic_enemies.py`. [Current import evidence](art/organic-enemies/animation/anatomy-import.json), [CPU preparation and reproduction](art/organic-enemies/animation/anatomy-review.md). `-FootMarkers` additionally marks measured claw-skin points during the Boss Slam in Editor; final Shipping reviews omit markers.

`scripts/Capture-PlayerMotion.ps1 -Build Shipping -CaptureDirectory <new-private-directory>` records 36 seconds of folded strikes, guard/charge/release/recall, dash, running and jump/landing. This opt-in `-DBMotionFrameStudy` uses the actual engine fixed frame rate, rejects a dt mismatch, and records after player/physics updates into Saved/PlayerMotionStudy. Every PNG corresponds to one actual 30 Hz tick; the CSV includes weapon transforms and attack recovery. It uses staged invulnerable practice targets and deliberate fixture relocation for isolated heavy/dash views. It is silent and does not establish native held-input behavior or an ordinary playthrough. The older audio-oriented `-DBMotionCapture` remains a separate sampled recorder.
