# Organic enemy increment

The player remains a cyborg; these enemies are biological. Briarhide is a broad, antlered predator with hide, horny ridges and hooked hands. MireSeer is a slim amphibian caster with webbed extremities, a pleated gill collar and an amber throat sac. These are original designs and provisional internal names, not settled antagonist lore.

The quality references inspected on September 10, 2026 were [Kena's character animation and creature appeal](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/the-magic-of-creating-kena-bridge-of-spirits?lang=en-US), [Horizon Forbidden West's habitat and environmental storytelling](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/11/22/horizon-forbidden-west-an-authentic-world/) and [Returnal's decisive movement](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/04/29/returnal-gameplay-dev-details-and-more-in-nearly-30-minutes-of-new-footage/). The application here is distinct biological silhouettes, continuous moving anatomy, purposeful habitat clusters and forceful evasion. No game assets or character designs were copied.

Original image_gen RGBA references and exact prompts are in `references/`. Both assets were generated locally with TRELLIS.2 at actual 1536 resolution and 4096-pixel PBR export. `generation-report.json` records seeds, revisions, timings, the retained first failure and recovery without lowering resolution. Large raw geometry, latents and finished sources remain under `<windowsOutputRoot>/organic-enemies/{briarhide,mire-seer}`; they are deliberately excluded from Git. Dependency terms and provenance remain in `AI3D.md` and `assets/manifest.csv`; this is an internal evaluation package.

`prepare_organic_enemy.py` uses each `rig/*.json` anatomical specification. The motion revision reopens the retained prepared Blender source, preserves its topology, UVs, colors, atlas bytes and original twelve rest matrices, then adds independent feet and hands for sixteen bones. It replaces the anatomical weights with at most four normalized influences. Runtime triangle counts in Blender remain 189,560 for Briarhide and 170,000 for MireSeer; the Briarhide FBX roundtrip reports 189,559, matching the v1 FBX. Its small non-emissive eyes and original pigment floor remain. The original `finished` folders are retained; revised FBX/Blender outputs and seven CPU stress views per creature are in `finished-motion`, selected by `output_folder` in each spec. [Preparation, pose and roundtrip reports](animation/Briarhide-prep-report.json) record the exact preservation and unit contract.

`import_organic_enemies.py` runs through the full Unreal editor, imports both skeletal meshes and PBR materials under `/Game/Art/OrganicEnemies`, and verifies scale and fifteen bone-parent links per creature. [Import report](animation/import.json). DBEnemy uses continuous poseable skin, measured locomotion, ground traces, world-space foot stance targets and two-bone limb solving. The caster reaches around the actual charge/projectile origin. Combat collision, attack phases and damage timing remain authoritative. The old visible robot components are retired.

The animation remains procedural, with no individual fingers/toes, facial rig or ragdoll. There is one runtime LOD per creature. Deep knee bends compress the inner joint, and large reaches can overlap the generated leafy surfaces. Wrist spikes and shin-driven toe distortion were corrected in CPU review. Complete actual-AI movement, hit and death captures are the acceptance evidence; the first engine pass exposed turn sliding, a rearward strike and a seated collapse and was rejected. See [current integration status](../../STATUS.md) and [independent animation review](../../evidence/enemy-animation-qa.md) for subsequent results. Blender previews and successful import alone do not establish visual quality or owner acceptance.

The current motion package is **0.5.1-motion1**, executable source `a0c7642a98002a40f6cc8369bc36085ac8a1f16d`. Six actual Shipping studies delivered 3,546 consecutive frames: full Melee/Caster/Boss paths, close player-camera combat and a Hunter landing. The late ground-skimming swing and restart body drop were corrected after the earlier packaged candidates failed review. [Exact build and coverage](../../evidence/enemy-animation-build.json), [35-second review](../../evidence/enemy-animation-review.mp4). The high-end indie target remains the governing quality requirement; these bounded corrections and the retained rig limitations do not establish a finished animation system.
