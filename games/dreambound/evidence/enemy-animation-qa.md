## Short visual review checklist

The criteria below were agreed before capture. The v1 review above now records partial actual coverage and failures; these rows are not an acceptance checklist with implied passes.

| Sequence | Evidence that matters |
| --- | --- |
| Start, walk/run, stop | At least two complete strides for Briarhide and MireSeer, including acceleration and stopping. Planted feet stay on recognizable paving features, swing feet clear the surface, knees bend consistently, and the body transfers weight. No treadmill gait, extended foot skating, sudden pelvis drop or foot shuffle after stopping. |
| Turn and lateral travel | Side/three-quarter view of a 90° and a large reversal, plus melee circling or caster retreat. Feet release and replant instead of twisting under a swiveling body; head/torso lead and recover coherently. Preserve the actual movement direction rather than showing a forward walk while translating sideways. |
| Melee attack | Complete approach → tell → commitment/contact → follow-through → recovery → movement. | Caster attack | Reposition/stop → complete tell → both projectile releases → recovery. The caster should have its own movement character, not the melee motion at a different scale. |
| Hit and death | Real non-practice front/side impact during travel or recovery, followed by a lethal hit and at least two seconds of settling. Collapse begins from the current pose, reaches the ground without major penetration/levitation, and settles without persistent jitter. |
